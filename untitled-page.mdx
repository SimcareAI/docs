 Blackboard–SimCare LTI Integration: Instructor, Builder, and Student Workflow<br /><br />

**1. Open SimCare from Blackboard using the Launch link** [0:13](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=13)

- In a Blackboard course, go to **Add Content** and open the **Content Market**.
- Select the SimCare option called **Launch SimCare**.
- This creates a simple link that lets instructors or students open SimCare in a new tab and be automatically logged in.
- After adding it, refresh the course page to see the new launch link.

 

**2. Add a SimCare assignment from the Content Market** [1:14](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=74)

- Go back to the **Content Market** and choose **SimCare Assignment Picker**.
- This shows assignments tied to your program, including ones created by instructors, course builders, subject matter experts, or others in the program.
- Select the assignment you want to use, such as **Assignment 3**.
- Refresh the Blackboard page to confirm the assignment has been added.

 

**3. Launch the assignment as an instructor for testing** [1:45](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=105)

- Click the added assignment link in Blackboard.
- It opens in a new tab and launches in **test mode** for the instructor.
- For this example, the assignment is a **case study** that asks the user to choose a client.
- This lets instructors verify the assignment flow before students use it.

 

**4. Student view: launch SimCare and start the assignment** [2:05](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=125)

- On the student side of the same Blackboard course, the student sees both:
  - the **Launch SimCare** link
  - the **assignment** link
- Clicking **Launch SimCare** logs the student into SimCare in a new tab.
- Clicking the assignment opens the actual assignment so the student can begin work.

 

**5. Complete the case conversation requirements** [2:41](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=161)

- The student must complete at least **four back-and-forth exchanges** with the client for the case to count.
- In the demo, the student talks with **Emma** several times.
- Once the conversation is sufficiently complete, the student can end the case.
- Ending the call marks the assignment as completed and starts feedback generation.

 

**6. Feedback is generated and sent back to the instructor** [3:24](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=204)

- After the student ends the conversation, SimCare begins generating feedback.
- That feedback is sent back to the instructor side for review and grading.
- The transcript notes that the grading details are handled in SimCare, not directly in Blackboard for this demo.

 

**7. Instructor reviews the submission in SimCare** [3:46](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=226)

- On the SimCare instructor side, a new class appears with the same name as the Blackboard course.
- The assignment copied from the master course is linked to the Blackboard course launch.
- The instructor can also see the student who completed the assignment, such as **John Doe**.
- From **View Submissions**, the instructor can open **Grade a Case** to review the work.

 

**8. Grade passback is not covered in this video** [4:23](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed?t=263)

- The video does **not** cover the full grade passback process.
- The intended flow is:
  - instructor enters a grade in SimCare
  - instructor clicks **Publish Grade**
  - grade passback sends the score back to Blackboard
- The team is still investigating:
  - the **zeroing issue**
  - the full **Blackboard Ultra grade passback** workflow
