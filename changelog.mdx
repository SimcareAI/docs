<Update label="November 13, 2026">

## Updates

- **Filter application progress by placement level and term.** The application progress grid now has **Placement level** and **Term** filters, plus a **Clear filters** button. Filters use the selections saved in each student's latest application, so students without a matching selection are hidden while a filter is on.
- **Breakout rooms in group cases.** For group cases that have breakout rooms enabled, you can choose which clients join a private conversation with you using **Create breakout room**. The others wait outside and can't hear you, and the feedback transcript marks when you moved between rooms. See [Talking to the avatar](/simulations/talking-to-the-avatar).

## Bug fixes

- **Clearance stepper uploads and saved steps.** An uploaded document no longer disappears and reappears in the clearance stepper, and a step a student just finished no longer shows as incomplete for a few minutes.

</Update>

<Update label="November 12, 2026">

## Updates

- **After-submission steps in the application overview.** The Application Process overview and the clearance step editor now list forms sent after submission below **Review & Submit**, so they're no longer numbered among the student's steps. See [Customization settings](/fieldx/step-0-2-customization-settings).
- **Open a Meet session from table view.** Clicking a session row in table view now opens it. To rename a session you own, use the **⋮** menu. See [Past sessions](/meet/past-sessions).
- **Date pickers in assign modals.** The assign form and assign report modals now use accessible date fields instead of native date inputs.

## Bug fixes

- **Student's own application form step.** Fixed a case where a student couldn't review or edit their own form step in a pre-application.
- **Custom clearance steps in application review.** Applications that skip the Sites step now show their custom steps, such as document uploads, in the review.
- **Older form attachments.** Fixed "Attachment not found" errors when opening files uploaded to forms before September 9, 2026.
- **Templates search.** Searching the Templates list from a later page now returns to the first page instead of showing "not found".
- **Delayed assignment emails and reset forms.** Delayed "New form to complete" emails are now sent reliably, and a reset form restores signers' drafts.

</Update>

<Update label="November 11, 2026">

## New features

- **Reset signatures only.** Next to **Reset entire form**, a new **Reset signatures only** action regenerates a form and keeps each role's previous answers, clearing only signatures and signing dates.
- **Remove a form from a group.** Inside an open form group, you can now remove a form from the group without deleting it.

## Updates

- **Clear students once.** A new program setting lets you clear students once: a student with an approved clearance can't pick or submit a different term or track. Updates and added sites or supervisors still work. See [Customization settings](/fieldx/step-0-2-customization-settings#settings).
- **Forms sent after submission.** In the clearance step editor, a form step can be set to send **After the student submits**. Students don't see it, and it never holds up their submission. Only forms the student takes no part in qualify.
- **Delayed assignment emails.** "New form to complete" emails can wait until a set number of days before the due date (default 7). Set it under **Notifications**.
- **Approval emails by placement level.** Choose who receives application approval emails for each placement level; a level with nobody chosen uses the program-wide list.
- **Pre-filled answers are labeled.** Application steps now show where a filled-in answer came from.
- **Student agreements carry across clearance cycles.** Agreements a student already completed are retained when they start a new clearance.

## Bug fixes

- **Approval emails when all admins are selected.** Selecting every admin for approval emails no longer results in nobody being emailed.
- **Scheduled recurring forms for several people.** Fixed schedules that assign a form to more than one person.
- **Silent pauses in feedback.** Feedback scoring now attributes the "did not respond" pauses to the student rather than the client.

</Update>

- **Cohort copies without due dates.** Copying a cohort now moves dates correctly when due dates are off and keeps early submission.

</Update>

<Update label="October 13, 2026">

## New features

- **Recorded session assignments.** Teachers can now assign a recorded session. Students record or upload a session in Meet, link it to the assignment, and answer any post-session questions. Teachers review the transcript, grade with a rubric, and release the result. See [Recorded session assignments](/meet/recorded-session-assignments).
- **Review session.** Open any recording to read its transcript, click a line to jump to that moment, search the transcript, and leave comments pinned to the video. Instructors can save comments to a library to reuse. See [Reviewing a session](/meet/reviewing-a-session).
- **Hide identifying details.** Transcripts can replace names, dates, places, and contact details with placeholders for reviewers and AI feedback. The session owner can still see the originals and can hide anything the detector missed. See [Hiding identifying details](/meet/privacy-and-redaction).
- **Schedule a meeting for someone else.** Program admins can schedule a session for a member of their program. The host runs it, and everyone is told that the admin will also have access to the recording. See [Scheduling & starting a meeting](/meet/scheduling-and-starting-a-meeting).
- **Program Oversight.** Program admins have a new tab to see how many recordings each student has, request access to them, and archive or permanently delete a departing student's recordings. See [Program Oversight](/meet/program-oversight).
- **Privacy & security.** A new page explains how Meet handles consent, access, auditing, and deletion. See [Privacy & security](/meet/privacy-and-security).

## Updates

- **Clearer recording and transcription notices.** The **AI Note Taker Active** badge now stays visible for the whole meeting, and the recording notice folds into a **Recording** badge after a few seconds.
- **Uploads ask for consent.** When you upload a session, you now name each client and confirm signed consent, and you can choose whether to transcribe it and hide identifying details.
- **Generate Feedback is temporarily unavailable** on Meet sessions while we improve it. Review session works as usual.
- **Student approval for admin viewing.** Students can approve or withdraw an admin's access to their recordings from an emailed link or from **Who can see my recordings**.

</Update>

<Update label="June 18, 2026">

## New features

- **Clinical case studies.** A new clinical case library is now available, with practice cases you can work through directly or launch as a full simulation. Each case opens to a Case Overview with the patient background, question count, and a one-click start. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can step away and pick up where you left off, and completed cases show your score the next time you open them.
- **Study packages.** The clinical case library now surfaces SimCare's NCMHCE study packages — Live Instruction, Self-Study, and Question Bundles — so you can browse package details without leaving the page.

## Updates

- **Smoother re-edits of submitted pre-applications.** When you reopen a previously submitted FieldX [pre-application](/fieldx/students/step-5-my-sites) to make changes, SimCare now creates a clean draft from your submitted version and carries your existing answers, forms, and assignments over to it. Your submitted record stays intact while you edit, and changes only replace it once you resubmit.
- **Licensure prep cases now connect to simulations.** When a clinical case has a matching simulation version, you can jump straight into the simulation from the score modal after answering the questions. If you arrive from a completed simulation, the score modal now offers a direct link back to your simulation feedback so you can compare the two side by side.

- **Smarter score review experience.** After you submit answers on a licensure prep case, the new score summary shows your total, lists each question with a correct/incorrect indicator, and lets you reopen the case in review mode to see your selections and explanations.
- **Your progress is saved as you go.** Answers on a licensure prep case now auto-save in the background, so you can leave and come back without losing your place. Reopening a completed attempt drops you straight into review mode with the score summary.

</Update>

<Update label="June 16, 2026">

## New features

- **In-session chat in Meets.** You can now send real-time text messages to everyone in a Meets session from a new chat panel in the meeting room. Useful when you want to share a code word, ask a therapist to pause, or pass along information silently without anyone in the room's physical surroundings overhearing.
  - Open the chat panel from the new chat button in the meeting control bar.
  - A badge on the button shows unread messages when the panel is closed, and resets when you open it.
  - Notifications are visual only — no sounds — so chats stay discreet.
  - Messages are visible to everyone in the meeting room and exist only for the duration of the session.
</Update>

<Update label="June 11, 2026">

## Bug fixes

- **FieldX supervisor invites are more reliable.** When you add a site supervisor in the [pre-application flow](/fieldx/students/step-5-my-sites), SimCare now catches a few edge cases that previously caused silent failures:
  - You can no longer invite your own email address as your supervisor. You'll see an inline error and a toast explaining the issue.
  - Inviting someone who already has a SimCare account in a conflicting role (student, teacher, faculty, or admin) now shows a clear, role-specific message so you know to use a different email.
  - Inviting someone whose existing account is unused or already a supervisor still upgrades smoothly, so legitimate invites go through without extra steps.
- In each blocked case, the invite modal stays open so you can correct the email and try again without losing your work.

</Update>
