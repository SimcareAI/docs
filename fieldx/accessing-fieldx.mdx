FieldX is available as **Field Experience** in the SimCare sidebar.

## Open Field Experience

1. Expand the left sidebar by moving your pointer over it.
2. Find **Field Experience** under **Site Placement** and click it.
3. FieldX opens the dashboard for your account and program role.

<Frame caption="Choose Field Experience in the sidebar. The account menu at the bottom left shows the signed-in user's role.">
  ![SimCare sidebar with red arrows pointing to Field Experience and the Student role in the account menu](/images/fieldx/audit/admin-account-role.png)
</Frame>

To check which account you are using, click your avatar or name at the bottom left. The account menu shows your name, email, and role.

## What's next

Visit the [FieldX overview](/fieldx/fieldx-overview) to choose the guide for your role.
