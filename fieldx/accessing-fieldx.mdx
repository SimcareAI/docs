title: "Accessing FieldX"
description: "How to open the sidebar and navigate to Field Experience from the SimCare dashboard."
---

FieldX lives in the left sidebar of the SimCare dashboard. Follow these steps to get there.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    From anywhere in SimCare, open the left sidebar. Your name and role appear at the top, followed by the **Admin Tools**, **FieldX**, and **Practice** sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Field Experience">
    Under the **FieldX** section, click **Field Experience** to open the FieldX portion of the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
