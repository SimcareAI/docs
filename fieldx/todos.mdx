The current FieldX dashboard does not show the **Todos** header button described in older guides. Use your cohort task lists to review formal assignments and your notes tools for personal or supervision notes.

## Find current tasks

For Program Admins, open **Cohorts** and choose **View All Tasks** to review work across cohorts. To focus on one class, click **Open Cohort** and use its **My Tasks** tab.

To create an evaluation, questionnaire, or timesheet-signing assignment, use [Creating & Assigning Tasks](/fieldx/step-4-create-assign-tasks). For repeated formal assignments, see [Recurring tasks](/fieldx/setting-up-recurring-tasks).

Students can review upcoming work in [Cohorts](/fieldx/students/step-4-the-cohorts-tab) and use [My Notes](/fieldx/students/step-6-helpful-tools-notes-to-dos) for reflections and notes.

## If your deployment shows Todos

The following instructions apply only when a **Todos** control is visible. The lightweight Todos panel is separate from formal cohort forms and reports.

| Tab | What's here |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Task** | Title, optional due date and description, assignees, and repeat settings |
| **Assigned to Me** | Pending todos assigned to you |
| **Assigned by Me** | Todos you created and their current status |
| **History** | Completed or dismissed todos involving you |

To create a todo:

1. Open **Create Task** and enter a **Title**.
2. Add a **Due Date** and **Description** if needed.
3. Select one or more people in **Assign To**.
4. Optionally turn on **Repeat**, choose **Repeat Until**, and select a daily, weekly, two-week, or monthly schedule.
5. Click **Create Task**. FieldX creates a separate task for each selected person.

Use **View Todo** to read the task. In **Assigned to Me**, choose **Complete** or **Dismiss** to move it out of the pending list and into **History**. In **Assigned by Me**, open a task to update its due date or the repeat settings of the original recurring task.
