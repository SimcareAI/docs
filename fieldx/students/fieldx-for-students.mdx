FieldX (also called Field Experience) is where you manage your practicum or internship: completing clearance, logging hours, tracking your placement sites, and staying on top of assigned forms and reports for your program.

## Creating your account

If you don't have a SimCare account yet:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to app.simcare.ai">
    Visit [app.simcare.ai](https://app.simcare.ai) and click **Create an account**.

    <Frame caption="The SimCare sign-in page, with Create an account highlighted.">
      ![Screenshot 2026 08 15 At 9 49 23 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-15-at-9.49.23-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 15 At 9 49 23 PM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter your first and last name, your **school email**, and a password, then agree to the terms.

    <Tip>
      Use your school email address. That's what associates your account with your university and unlocks FieldX.
    </Tip>

    <Frame caption="The Create your account form.">
      ![Screenshot 2026 08 15 At 9 50 04 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-15-at-9.50.04-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 08 15 At 9 50 04 PM")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your school email">
    Since your school uses FieldX, SimCare sends a 6-digit code to your school email before your account is created. Enter the code to finish signing up — you're automatically enrolled in your program.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Need details on SSO, magic-link sign-in, or two-factor authentication? See [Creating your account](/simulations/creating-an-account) for the full walkthrough.
</Tip>
