The **My Dashboard** tab is your home base in FieldX. It shows your overall status, surfaces what needs attention, and gives you an AI-generated summary of your recent activity.

## Opening it

Click **My Dashboard** in your Field Experience tab bar (it's the last tab). Unlike the other student tabs, My Dashboard is available even before clearance is approved — it shows you what's still pending.

## The two segments

My Dashboard has two segments you can switch between at the top:

- **Profile Details** _(default)_ — clearance, insurance, assigned forms, timesheet reports
- **Activity** — hours analytics, donut chart, AI activity summary

## Profile Details

This is the first place to look if something feels stuck or incomplete.

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Clearance Tasks** | Your overall clearance status and a shortcut to complete any outstanding items |
| **Insurance Documents** | Liability insurance documents and expiration details, when your program requires insurance |
| **Assigned Forms** | Forms across all your cohorts that need your action |
| **Timesheet Reports** | Hour-log reports — your status (pending your signature, signed, waiting on supervisor) |

Select a card to open its details. **Assigned Forms** and **Timesheet Reports** can show a cohort picker before the task list. The **Insurance Documents** card appears only when insurance is part of your program's requirements.

## Activity

Select **Activity** to view your **Hours Snapshot** and **AI Summary** side by side.

<Frame caption="Choose the level and term, export the snapshot, or refresh your AI Summary.">
  ![Activity dashboard with red arrows pointing to the term selector, Export, and Refresh](/images/fieldx/audit/student-activity.png)
</Frame>

### Hours snapshot (donut chart)

A donut chart shows logged hours broken down into **Direct**, **Indirect**, and **Supervision**, with totals below. Your program's rules determine which supervision hours count toward requirements.

### Level and term selectors

Select the placement level and term you want to review. Depending on your placement history and program, you may also see all-time or combined scope options. Switching scope updates both the snapshot and its AI summary.

### CSV export

Click **Export** to download a CSV of the selected snapshot: category totals, counted supervision, required hours, and scope details. For a row-by-row list of hour entries, run **Hours Log** from [Run Reports](/fieldx/students/step-7-running-reports).

### AI activity summary

A natural-language summary of your recent activity — useful for supervision check-ins, end-of-term reflection, or just stepping back and seeing where your time has gone. The summary generates when you open Activity with logged hours in the selected scope.

- Click **Refresh** at any time to regenerate it
- The summary changes when you switch term scopes — so each term gets its own reflection

<Tip>
  The AI summary is a good starting point for a supervision conversation. Paste it into a journal entry or supervision note to keep your reflection in one place.
</Tip>

## What's not here

A few things you might expect on a dashboard live elsewhere in FieldX:

- **Log hours** — Timesheet tab
- **Enroll in a cohort** — Cohorts tab → Enroll in Cohort button
- **Pending forms / reports** — Cohorts tab (or the Profile Details cards on My Dashboard)
- **Add a site** — My Sites tab
- **My Notes** — header button on the Timesheet tab

<Note>
  If My Dashboard shows clearance as still pending and you're sure you submitted, check My Sites and the email you used to sign up — a missing site or supervisor can hold up approval.
</Note>
