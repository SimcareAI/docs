Once your clearance is approved, the Field Experience page becomes your main hub. You'll see five tabs across the top:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Timesheet** | Log hours, view past entries, and see your approved vs. unapproved breakdown — see [Logging & Tracking Your Hours](/fieldx/students/step-3-the-timesheet-tab-logging-hours) |
| **Cohorts** | Class assignments, pending tasks, forms, reports, and your learning contract — see [Cohorts, Forms & Reports](/fieldx/students/step-4-the-cohorts-tab) |
| **My Sites** | View and manage your placement sites, add new ones, and manage supervisors — see [Managing Site Information](/fieldx/students/step-5-my-sites) |
| **Run Reports** | Generate reports scoped to your own data — see [Running Reports](/fieldx/students/step-7-running-reports) |
| **My Dashboard** | Your overview: clearance status, insurance documents, assigned forms, reports, and hours activity — see [My Dashboard](/fieldx/students/my-dashboard) |

<Note>
  Some tabs — particularly **Cohorts** — show a blocked overlay until your clearance is fully approved. If you see a lock screen, check your status on [Getting Started & Clearance Tasks](/fieldx/students/step-1-getting-started-and-clearance-tasks) before troubleshooting anything else.
</Note>

## Where to find pending tasks

The **Cohorts** tab is the best single view of everything you still need to do — its upcoming tasks panel surfaces all pending assigned forms and reports together, with urgency flags for items due soon.

**My Dashboard** shows the same items split by category (Assigned Forms, Timesheet Reports), which is useful if you want to focus on one type at a time.

<Tip>
  If you received an email about a form or report needing your attention, click the button in the email — it links directly to that item.
</Tip>

## Two lightweight tools

Two small tools live on the Timesheet tab's header and are available throughout FieldX:

- **Todos** — personal reminders, plus the ability to send reminders to others
- **My Notes** — private journaling, or supervision notes tied to a specific session

See [Notes & To-Dos](/fieldx/students/step-6-helpful-tools-notes-to-dos) for the full walkthrough.

## Something not working?

Most issues on any tab trace back to one cause: **your clearance isn't approved yet**. Check [Getting Started & Clearance Tasks](/fieldx/students/step-1-getting-started-and-clearance-tasks) for your status first — each tab's own page has a full troubleshooting section for anything more specific.
