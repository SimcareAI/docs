Use **My Notes** on the Timesheet header to keep journal entries and review supervision notes. Your **My Dashboard → Activity** view also provides an AI-generated summary of your logged hours.

## My Notes

Open **My Notes**, then choose one of its two tabs:

- **Journals** — create and manage your journal entries.
- **Supervision Notes** — review supervision notes shared with you by your supervisor.

<Frame caption="Start a journal with New Entry, or switch to Supervision Notes to review notes from your supervisor.">
  ![My Notes modal with red arrows pointing to New Entry and the Supervision Notes tab](/images/fieldx/audit/student-notes.png)
</Frame>

### Create a journal entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an entry">
    In **Journals**, select **New Entry**. If you have no entries yet, you can also select **Create Your First Entry**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your reflection">
    Add an optional title, check **Entry Date**, and fill in the required **Content** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose visibility and save">
    Scroll to **Visibility**, choose who can see the entry, and select **Create Entry**. Existing entries can be opened for editing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The visibility options are:

| Visibility | Who can see the entry |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | Only you |
| **Supervisor** | You and your supervisors |
| **Faculty** | You, faculty, and supervisors |
| **Everyone** | Everyone in your program |

Check visibility before saving, especially when your entry contains personal reflections or supervision details.

### Review supervision notes

Select **Supervision Notes** to read notes submitted by your supervisor. If the tab says **No supervision notes yet**, your supervisor has not shared any notes here. This tab is separate from the journals you create yourself.

## Finding tasks and reminders

The current FieldX header does not show **Todos**. Open **Cohorts** to find your assigned forms and reports. **My Notes** is for journal entries and supervision notes; it does not create reminders.

### Todos in older interfaces

If your deployment still shows a **Todos** button, open it to manage reminders. The tool has **Create Task**, **Assigned to Me**, **Assigned by Me**, and **History** tabs. The header badge shows open todos assigned to you.

Use **Create Task** to enter a task title, select its assignee, and add the available scheduling details. You can make a personal reminder or assign a reminder to someone in the available list. Creating a reminder for someone else sends them a task, so review the recipient before submitting.

A reminder is separate from a formal assignment. Complete your program's forms and reports from **Cohorts**, even if you also track their deadlines in a reminder tool.

## Clearance Tasks shortcut

The **Clearance Tasks** button in the header gives you one-click access to your clearance status — useful even after approval if you need to upload a renewed insurance form or update a supervisor.

## AI activity summary

Your **My Dashboard** tab has an AI-generated **activity summary** that reflects on your recent hours, supervision patterns, and learning trajectory. See [My Dashboard](/fieldx/students/my-dashboard) for the full breakdown.

The summary generates when you open Activity with logged hours in the selected scope. Click **Refresh** to regenerate it any time. It's a useful prompt for end-of-term reflections, supervision check-ins, or your own self-assessment.
