The **My Sites** tab shows all the placement sites where you're doing your fieldwork. This is where you manage the locations where you're logging hours — you can view active sites, add established program sites, and track pending approvals.

Some programs turn off **Request New Site** on this tab. If you don't see that button, it isn't a bug — your program only lets students propose a brand-new site from [Clearance Tasks](/fieldx/students/step-1-getting-started-and-clearance-tasks), not from My Sites. You can still pick an established site here.

## Viewing your sites

Each site card shows:

- Site name, location, and description
- Your supervisor(s) at that site
- Any site-specific requirements or notes

Click a site card to open its full profile, where you can see more detail and take actions like canceling a pending request.

## Selecting an established site

Your program maintains a list of pre-approved sites. Browse the list and select one that matches where you're doing your placement.

<Tip>
  Established sites don't require Program Admin approval — once you select one, it's immediately active and you can log hours there right away.
</Tip>

This is the fastest path to getting started. If your site is in the established list, use it.

## Requesting a new site

If your placement site isn't in the established list, you can submit a request for it to be added and approved — either from this tab, or from Clearance Tasks, depending on how your program is set up.

### If you see Request New Site

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the request">
    Click **Request New Site** on the My Sites tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide site details">
    Fill in the basic information about the site: name, location, and a brief description. Include your supervisor's name and contact email so your Program Admin can verify the placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Submit the request. Your coordinator will review it and either approve or reach out with questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If Request New Site is missing

Your program has disabled student site requests on My Sites (and in Log Hours). Established sites still work here — browse the list and add one. To propose a site that isn't in that list, go back to **Clearance Tasks**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Clearance Tasks">
    From your Field Experience dashboard, click **Clearance Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you're already approved, start an update">
    On the **Application Approved** card, click **Update Application**, then **I Understand, Continue**. You keep your current access while the update is reviewed. If you're still in your original clearance flow, skip this step and go straight to **Select Sites**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Propose the site on Select Sites">
    On the **Select Sites** step, click **Propose Site** (or **Propose New Site** if the list is empty). Fill in the site details and submit for approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Clearance is the only place a new-site request still works when your program has turned this off on My Sites. Don't wait for a **Request New Site** button to appear on this tab — it won't. See [Updating an approved application](/fieldx/students/step-1-getting-started-and-clearance-tasks#updating-an-approved-application) if you need the full update flow.
</Note>

While the request is pending, the site card shows a **Pending** badge. Once your coordinator approves it, the badge disappears and the site becomes active — you can then log hours there.

<Warning>
  Do not log hours at a site that still shows a Pending badge. Those hours may not count toward your requirements if the site is ultimately not approved.
</Warning>

## Canceling a pending site request

If you submitted a request by mistake, chose the wrong site, or your placement changed:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the pending site">
    On the My Sites tab, locate the site card showing the **Pending** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the site detail">
    Click the card to open the site's detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the request">
    Click **Cancel request**. The request is removed immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After canceling, you can select an established site instead, or submit a new request from **Request New Site** if you have it — otherwise go back to **Clearance Tasks** and propose the correct site there.

## Adding a supervisor to a site

Your supervisor needs to be in the system before they can sign off on your hours. If they don't appear in the supervisor dropdown when you're logging hours, add them from **My Sites** (no Tools menu needed) or from the Timesheet Tools menu — unless your program has turned those actions off.

### From My Sites (no Tools menu)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site card">
    On the **My Sites** tab, find the placement site and look at the **My Supervisor** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add">
    Click **Add** next to that heading. If the site already has supervisors in the program, you can pick one. If not, the invite form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite if needed">
    Enter their name and email, then submit. They get an invitation email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From the Timesheet Tools menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Timesheet tab">
    Navigate to the **Timesheet** tab from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Tools menu">
    Click the three-dots icon in the timesheet toolbar to open the **Tools** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add Supervisor">
    Choose **Add Supervisor** from the menu. Enter their name and email, then submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once they accept and join, they'll appear in your supervisor dropdown when logging hours.

<Note>
  Adding a supervisor here makes them available across all your sites — you don't need to add them separately per site. **Switch Supervisor** in the Timesheet Tools menu stays available even when **Add Supervisor** is hidden; it only switches among supervisors already linked to you.
</Note>

### If Add and Add Supervisor are missing

Some programs turn off student supervisor invites on My Sites and in Timesheet Tools. That isn't a bug. To invite someone new, go back to **Clearance Tasks**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Clearance Tasks">
    From your Field Experience dashboard, click **Clearance Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you're already approved, start an update">
    On the **Application Approved** card, click **Update Application**, then **I Understand, Continue**. You keep your current access while the update is reviewed. If you're still in your original clearance flow, skip this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite on Add Supervisors">
    On the **Add Supervisors** step (titled **Select Supervisor(s)**), click **Add new**, fill in their name, email, and credentials, then click **Invite Site Supervisor**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  See [Updating an approved application](/fieldx/students/step-1-getting-started-and-clearance-tasks#updating-an-approved-application) for the full update flow.
</Note>

### Email addresses you can't invite

To protect existing accounts, the supervisor invite form blocks a few cases. If you see an inline error and the modal stays open, check the email you entered:

- **Your own email.** You can't add yourself as a supervisor. Enter your supervisor's email instead.
- **An email already used by a student, faculty member, teacher, or admin.** That account already has a different role in SimCare and can't be converted into a supervisor. Ask your supervisor for a personal email address, or contact your coordinator to resolve the conflict.

Free SimCare accounts and existing supervisor accounts work normally — they'll be linked to your site automatically.

## Common issues

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| My site isn't in the established list | If you see **Request New Site**, use that button. If you don't, go to **Clearance Tasks** (use **Update Application** if you're already approved) and propose the site on the **Select Sites** step. |
| I don't see a Request New Site button | Your program disabled new-site requests on My Sites. You can still add an established site from the list. To request a brand-new site, go back to **Clearance Tasks** → **Select Sites** and use **Propose Site**. |
| My site has been Pending for a long time | Contact your coordinator directly — they may need additional information to approve it. |
| I requested the wrong site | Open the site card with the Pending badge, click into it, and use **Cancel request**. Then resubmit with the correct site. |
| My supervisor doesn't appear in the dropdown when logging hours | On the site card, click **Add** next to **My Supervisor**. Or use Timesheet \> Tools (three-dots) \> **Add Supervisor**. If both are missing, go to **Clearance Tasks** (use **Update Application** if you're already approved) → **Add Supervisors** → **Add new**. |
| I don't see Add next to My Supervisor | Your program hid student supervisor invites on My Sites (and usually **Add Supervisor** in Timesheet Tools too). Invite them from **Clearance Tasks** → **Add Supervisors** → **Add new**. |
| My supervisor was added but still isn't showing up | They haven't accepted the invitation yet. Ask them to check their email (including spam) for the invite. |
| I can't log hours at my site | Check that the site is active (no Pending badge). Also confirm your clearance is approved — go to My Dashboard \> Profile Details \> Clearance Tasks. Hour logging requires clearance approval. |
| I selected an established site but it doesn't appear on my list | Refresh the page. If it still doesn't appear, contact your coordinator — there may be a configuration issue with that site. |
