The **My Sites** tab shows all the placement sites where you're doing your fieldwork. This is where you manage the locations where you're logging hours — you can view active sites, add established program sites, and track pending approvals.

If you see **Request New Site** on the **My Sites** tab, click it to propose a site that isn't in the established list.

## Viewing your sites

Each site card shows:

- Site name, location, and description
- Your supervisor(s) at that site
- Any site-specific requirements or notes

Click a site card to open its full profile, where you can see more detail and take actions like canceling a pending request.

## Selecting an established site

Select **Program Sites** to browse your program's established sites. Search or filter the list, open a site to review its details, and select **Add to My Sites** for the correct placement.

<Tip>
  Many programs activate established sites immediately. Some require approval even when the site is already in Program Sites. Check the resulting status in **My Sites** and wait for approval if it shows **Pending**.
</Tip>

This is the fastest path to getting started. If your site is in the established list, use it.

## Requesting a new site

If your placement site isn't in the established list and you see **Request New Site**, click that button on the **My Sites** tab to propose it.

### If you see Request New Site

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the request">
    Click **Request New Site** on the My Sites tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search before creating a site">
    Search by **Site Name or Address**. If you find the correct site, select it and review its details. If it is missing, select **Create New Site** or **Skip and Create New Site**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the site details">
    Use **Next** to work through Basic Information, Site Details, and the remaining sections. Complete the required contact and placement information shown by the wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Check the **Review & Submit** page, then select **Submit Request**. Your coordinator reviews the request unless your program automatically approves it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While the request is pending, the site card shows a **Pending** badge. Once your coordinator approves it, the badge disappears and the site becomes active — you can then log hours there.

<Warning>
  Do not log hours at a site that still shows a Pending badge. Those hours may not count toward your requirements if the site is ultimately not approved.
</Warning>

## Canceling a pending site request

If you submitted a request by mistake, chose the wrong site, or your placement changed:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the pending site">
    On the My Sites tab, locate the site card showing the **Pending** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the site detail">
    Click the card to open the site's detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the request">
    Click **Cancel request**. The request is removed immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After canceling, you can select an established site instead, or click **Request New Site**, if available, to submit a new request with the correct site details.

## Adding a supervisor to a site

Your supervisor needs to be in the system before they can sign off on your hours. If they don't appear in the supervisor dropdown when you're logging hours, add them from **My Sites** or from the Timesheet **Actions** menu — unless your program has turned those actions off.

### From My Sites

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site card">
    On the **My Sites** tab, find the placement site and look at the **My Supervisor** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add">
    Click **Add** next to that heading. If the site already has supervisors in the program, you can pick one. If not, the invite form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite if needed">
    Select the correct site and enter the required supervisor details, including their name and email, then submit. They get an invitation email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From the Timesheet Actions menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Timesheet tab">
    Navigate to the **Timesheet** tab from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    Click **Actions** in the timesheet toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add Supervisor">
    Choose **Add Supervisor** from the menu. Select the correct site and enter the required supervisor details, including their name and email, then submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After adding the supervisor, check the **Supervisor** dropdown for the selected site when logging hours. New invitees also need to follow their invitation email to access their supervisor account.

<Note>
  Supervisor choices are linked to the selected site. If you use multiple sites, check the supervisor connection for each one. **Switch Supervisor** in the Timesheet **Actions** menu stays available even when **Add Supervisor** is hidden; it switches among supervisors already linked to you.
</Note>

### If Add and Add Supervisor are missing

Some programs turn off student supervisor invites on My Sites and in Timesheet Actions. That isn't a bug. To invite someone new, go back to **Clearance Tasks**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Clearance Tasks">
    From your Field Experience dashboard, click **Clearance Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you're already approved, start an update">
    On the **Application Approved** card, click **Update Application**, then **I Understand, Continue**. You keep your current access while the update is reviewed. If you're still in your original clearance flow, skip this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite on Add Supervisors">
    On the **Add Supervisors** step (titled **Select Supervisor(s)**), click **Add new**, fill in their name, email, and credentials, then click **Invite Site Supervisor**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  See [Updating an approved application](/fieldx/students/step-1-getting-started-and-clearance-tasks#updating-an-approved-application) for the full update flow.
</Note>

### Who you can add as a supervisor

Admin and faculty accounts can be added as supervisors using their existing email addresses.

Students can only be added as supervisors if their admin has enabled special settings that allow it. If you're a student hoping to add another student (for example, a postdoc) as your supervisor, reach out to your field experience coordinator.

You can't add yourself as a supervisor. Enter your supervisor's email instead.

Free SimCare accounts and existing supervisor accounts work normally — they'll be linked to your site automatically.

## Common issues

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| My site isn't in the established list | If you see **Request New Site**, click it to propose the site. |
| My site has been Pending for a long time | Contact your coordinator directly — they may need additional information to approve it. |
| I requested the wrong site | Open the site card with the Pending badge, click into it, and use **Cancel request**. Then resubmit with the correct site. |
| My supervisor doesn't appear in the dropdown when logging hours | On the site card, click **Add** next to **My Supervisor**. Or use Timesheet \> **Actions** \> **Add Supervisor**. If both are missing, go to **Clearance Tasks** (use **Update Application** if you're already approved) → **Add Supervisors** → **Add new**. |
| I don't see Add next to My Supervisor | Your program hid student supervisor invites on My Sites (and usually **Add Supervisor** in Timesheet Actions too). Invite them from **Clearance Tasks** → **Add Supervisors** → **Add new**. |
| My supervisor was added but still isn't showing up | Check that you selected the same site used when adding them, then refresh. Ask them to check their invitation email if they also cannot access their supervisor account. |
| I can't log hours at my site | Check that the site is active (no Pending badge). Also confirm your clearance is approved — go to My Dashboard \> Profile Details \> Clearance Tasks. Hour logging requires clearance approval. |
| I selected an established site but it doesn't appear on my list | Refresh the page. If it still doesn't appear, contact your coordinator — there may be a configuration issue with that site. |
