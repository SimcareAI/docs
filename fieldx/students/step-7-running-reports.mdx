---

The **Run Reports** tab lets you generate reports using your own logged data — a quick way to check your progress or pull together your field experience activity for a supervisor or advisor.

<Frame caption="The Run Reports tab, showing available reports.">
  ![Run Reports tab listing available report types](/images/fieldx-run-reports.jpg)
</Frame>

## Available reports

Depending on what your program has enabled, you might see reports like:

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly Activity Ledger** | A week-by-week summary of your logged hours |
| **Multi-Week Hours Progress** | Your hours logged over a longer stretch of time |
| **Hours Trend Chart** | A visual chart of how your hours trend over time |
| **Complete Hours Entry Log** | Every hour entry you've logged, in one list |

<Tip>
  You can also generate a Run Report directly from the Timesheet tab — click **Create Timesheet Report** in the header buttons.
</Tip>

## Running a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the report">
    Choose the report you want from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview it">
    Click **Preview** to see the information it will include before you generate it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the report">
    Click **Run Report** to generate it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These reports can help you review your own progress and field experience activity over time.

<Tip>
  See also: [My Dashboard](/fieldx/students/my-dashboard) for your overall progress at a glance.
</Tip>
