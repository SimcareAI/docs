Welcome to FieldX! Before you can log hours or access your placement, you need to complete the **Clearance Process**. This is a one-time set of steps your program requires before you can participate in supervised field experience.

## The Welcome page

The first time you click **Field Experience** in the sidebar, you'll land on the **Welcome to FieldX** page (at `/fieldx-get-started`). The welcome page shows three step cards:

1. **Complete Paperwork** — submit your clearance application
2. **Get Approved** — your Program Admin reviews and approves your application
3. **Start Field Experience** — log hours and complete cohort tasks

At the bottom there's a **Start Paperwork** button. Click it to begin.

<Note>
  If you've already started clearance, the page auto-redirects you straight to the clearance flow. You'll only see the welcome screen once.
</Note>

## Why is my Field Experience page locked?

If you skip the welcome screen and click straight into a Field Experience tab, you'll see a locked screen. This is expected — it means your pre-application (clearance) hasn't been approved yet. Almost every action in FieldX — logging hours, accessing your cohort, viewing placement details — requires clearance approval first.

<Note>
  If you see tabs like **Discover**, **Presenting Issues**, or **My Courses** instead of a locked screen, you're in the SimCare Simulations area, not FieldX. Go back to the main navigation and click **Field Experience** to reach FieldX.
</Note>

Once your clearance is approved by your Program Admin, the lock lifts and stays lifted for that placement.

<Note>
  If your Program Admin later moves you to a new placement (a different level such as Practicum → Internship, or a different track type), your program may require re-clearance for the new placement. You'll see a blocked screen explaining the change, with a button to complete the clearance tasks again. Your uploaded sites and insurance carry over; you'll only need to redo handbook and agreement steps.
</Note>

### New-term clearance reminders

Some programs require students to redo clearance at the start of each new term. If your program has this turned on:

- If you're **not currently in an active placement** when the new term starts, your Field Experience re-locks with a message like _"A new term has started. Please complete clearance tasks again for this term to continue."_ Complete the flow again to unlock.
- If you're **already in an active Practicum or Internship** when the new term starts, you're **not** blocked. You keep logging hours as usual, and you'll see a small red dot on the **Clearance Tasks** button as a reminder to complete the new term's clearance when you have time.

## Multi-person forms & signers

Some clearance steps aren't finished when you submit — they need one or more other people (your supervisor, faculty, a school principal, a preceptor) to complete their portion before the step is done. These are **multi-person forms**. You'll see this flow inside **Clearance Tasks** whenever a step is labeled with more than one required role, and you'll see the same waiting screen and controls again if you [update an approved application](#updating-an-approved-application).

### Choosing your signers

When you open a multi-person step, the form shows a **Signers** section above (or in place of) the form fields. For each required role, one of the following appears:

| Signer type | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Supervisor** | Search your placement site's approved supervisors and select one. If none exist yet, use the **Add a supervisor to your site** link to invite them from My Sites first, then come back. |
| **Faculty / Teacher** | Pick from the faculty assigned to your track. You cannot enter faculty manually — if your list is empty, contact your Program Admin. |
| **Program Admin** | Pick from the Program Admins in your track. Manual entry is not available. |
| **Custom signer** (for example _Principal_, _Preceptor_) | Enter the person's full name and email. They don't need a SimCare account — they'll get a link by email. |

Some steps have **fixed custom signers** pre-set by your Program Admin (for example a specific district contact). In that case you'll see the name and email displayed as read-only — you don't need to enter anything.

<Note>
  If a supervisor role has candidates you don't recognize, click **Enter manually** to type in a name and email instead. Faculty and admin roles are locked to the list your program provides.
</Note>

### Submitting the form

After you fill in your section and pick your signers, click **Submit & Notify Signers**. SimCare emails each signer a link to the section they own. You'll then land on the **Waiting for signer(s)** screen, which lists each site, each signer, whether they've completed their portion, and when their invitation was sent.

<Note>
  You can move on to other clearance steps while you wait, but you can't submit your final application until every required signer has completed their part.
</Note>

The waiting page refreshes automatically as signers finish, so you don't need to reload it manually.

### Multi-site (staggered) forms

If your placement includes more than one site and the step requires a signer per site, you'll see a **stepper** at the top showing one entry per site (for example _Site 1 of 3_). Fill the form out for the current site, submit it, and the stepper advances to the next site.

<Tip>
  You'll sign the same form once per site. Your answers for one site don't carry into the next — each site can have different responses and different signers.
</Tip>

### Fixing signers after you submit

From the **Waiting for signer(s)** screen, each pending signer row has three actions:

- **Resend email** — sends a fresh invitation link to the signer. Use this if they say they never received it or the link expired.
- **Change signer** — replace the pending signer with a different person. For supervisor slots you can pick a different supervisor from your site or enter a new name/email; for custom slots you enter a new name/email. The old invitation is invalidated and a new one is sent.
- **Clear signer** — remove the signer from this form. If they're the only signer on that form instance, the whole instance is reset; you'll be warned before this happens.

Signers who have already completed their portion can't be changed or cleared — their submission stays locked in.

### Removing an instance you don't need

On staggered multi-site forms, if a signer at one of your sites doesn't actually need to complete this form (for example one placement doesn't require this signature), open that site's step and click **This signer doesn't need to complete this form — remove it**. The instance is removed and the stepper collapses to the remaining sites.

This action is only available on instances where no signer has finished yet.

### Starting over

If you need to change something structural — a different set of signers, a different site — use **Start Over** (on required steps) or **Clear & Continue** (on optional steps) at the top of the form:

- **Start Over** wipes your answers and every signer invitation for this step, so you can pick signers again from scratch. Signers who already completed their part will need to redo it.
- **Clear & Continue** discards this optional step entirely and moves you on.

<Warning>
  Both actions notify any completed signers that their previous submission no longer counts. Only use them when you actually need to restart — for a small typo, use **Change signer** or **Update Application** instead.
</Warning>

### External signing pages

Some form templates open in a full-page signing experience instead of inline in the clearance flow. When you see one of those:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Open Form**. The form opens in a new page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your section and submit">
    Fill in the form and submit it there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return and refresh">
    Come back to the clearance step and click **Refresh Status**. The status chip flips from _Waiting for Submission_ to _Form Completed_ once your submission is picked up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Reviewing and editing your submission

Once you've submitted, use **View Submission** (or **Review / Edit** after everyone finishes) on the waiting screen to see exactly what you sent for each site. Editing here creates a corrected copy — your Program Admin will need to re-approve the change, the same as with [Update Application](#updating-an-approved-application).

### Common signer issues

**My supervisor never got the email.** Ask them to check spam, then use **Resend email** on the signer's row. If they still don't see it, use **Change signer** to re-enter their email in case there's a typo.

**I picked the wrong faculty member.** Use **Change signer** on that row and pick the correct person from the list. The wrong signer's invitation link stops working.

**A signer completed their part but I need to change my answers.** Use **Update Application** from the Clearance Tasks page, not **Start Over**. Update Application carries completed signatures forward so nobody has to redo their work unless you actually change their section.

**The stepper shows a site that shouldn't need this form.** Open that site's step and click **This signer doesn't need to complete this form — remove it**. If the button isn't there, a signer has already completed the form for that site — use **Change signer** or **Clear signer** instead.

## After approval

Once approved, the lock lifts and your full Field Experience dashboard opens. You can:

- Start logging hours in the **Timesheet** tab
- See cohort assignments in the **Cohorts** tab
- View and manage your placement in **My Sites**
- See your overall progress in **My Dashboard**

## Updating an approved application

If something about your placement changes after your application is approved — you switched supervisors, added a new site, or your Program Admin asked you to correct a detail — you can update your application without starting over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Clearance Tasks">
    From your dashboard, click **Clearance Tasks**. You'll see the **Application Approved** card with two options: **View Application** and **Update Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Application">
    A confirmation dialog explains that updating starts a new copy of your application pre-filled with your current answers, and that your Program Admin will need to re-approve the changes before they take effect. Click **I Understand, Continue** to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change only what you need to">
    You're placed at the first step of the clearance flow with every answer already filled in. Move through the steps and edit anything that needs updating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resubmit for review">
    On the Finalize step, click **Submit Application** again. Your Program Admin will review the changes and either approve or reject the update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your originally approved application stays active while your update is pending review, so you keep your full Field Experience access the whole time. Nothing switches over until the update is approved.
</Note>

**Multi-person forms carry forward.** Any signature or form step you already completed with a supervisor or third party (for example a signed site agreement) is carried forward as already-complete — you don't need to collect signatures again unless you change something on that step. Steps you never finished the first time reset to blank so you can complete them fresh. See [Multi-person forms & signers](#multi-person-forms-signers) above for the full signer flow.

<Tip>
  See also: [Navigating the Field Experience Dashboard](/fieldx/students/step-2-the-field-experience-dashboard) for a tour of the tabs you'll use once you're cleared.
</Tip>

## Common issues

**My clearance application looks stuck.** Check the top of the Clearance Tasks page for a status banner. If it says "Awaiting Approval," your Program Admin hasn't reviewed it yet. If it says "Rejected," scroll down to read their feedback and re-submit after correcting.

**I can't find my site or supervisor in the list.** On the **Select Sites** step, click **Propose Site** to add a placement that isn't already in your program's established list. If you're already approved and don't see **Request New Site** on My Sites, that's expected for some programs — come back here, click **Update Application**, and propose the site on **Select Sites**. For supervisors, use the **Add Supervisor** form to invite them — they'll get an email and can complete their profile once they accept.

**The lock screen won't go away even though I submitted everything.** The lock only lifts once your Program Admin approves. Check the My Dashboard → Profile Details → Clearance Tasks card for your current status.
