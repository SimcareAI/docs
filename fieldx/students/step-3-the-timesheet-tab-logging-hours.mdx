The Timesheet tab is where you spend most of your time in FieldX. It's where you log hours, track progress toward your requirements, and manage past entries. Every hour you log here feeds directly into your supervisor's reports and your program's graduation requirements — so accuracy matters.

It's also the **default tab** you land on when you open Field Experience after clearance is approved.

<Frame caption="Select Hours Details to review your progress, or Add Hour Logs to record hours.">
  ![Timesheet tab with red arrows pointing to Hours Details and Add Hour Logs](/images/fieldx/students/timesheet-annotated.png)
</Frame>

## The header buttons

Across the top of the Timesheet tab you'll find quick-access buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **My Notes**_(orange)_ | Create journal entries with a chosen visibility and review supervision notes |
| **Clearance Tasks** | Jump back to your clearance flow (insurance renewals, supervisor updates) |
| **Support** | Open the available help and support options |

## Logging hours

<Warning>
  You can only log hours after your clearance is approved. If logging is blocked, check **My Dashboard → Profile Details → Clearance Tasks** for your status.
</Warning>

<Frame caption="Choose the cohort and activity type, then select the site for this entry.">
  ![Log Hours modal with red arrows pointing to Cohort, Activity Type, and Site](/images/fieldx/audit/student-log-manual.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Timesheet tab">
    Click the **Timesheet** tab from your Field Experience dashboard. Use the **Viewing:** dropdown above the totals to choose the term for your hours summary. The table has separate date and filter controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Hour Logs">
    Click **Add Hour Logs** in the toolbar, then use **Manual Entry**.

    If this is your first time logging hours, the one-time **Timesheet Logging Agreement** modal appears. Read it carefully and click **Accept** to continue — you can't log hours until you accept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the entry">
    The modal asks for:

    - **Activity Type** — what were you doing? (e.g., Direct Service, Supervision, Documentation)
    - **Date** — when did it happen?
    - **Start & end time** — duration is calculated automatically
    - **Site** — which placement site were you at?
    - **Supervisor** — who supervised this activity?
    - **Cohort** — which class should these hours count toward?
    - **Additional Notes** _(optional)_ — additional context about the session

    The form may pre-fill selections from your last entry. Check each value before saving. For an eligible university-supervision activity, the form uses your cohort's faculty supervisor and does not require a placement site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the entry">
    Click **Log Hours** at the bottom of the modal. The entry appears in your timesheet table as **Unapproved** until the required approval process is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reusing a weekly pattern

After you have enough consistent logging history, the manual-entry form may show **Draft this week from your pattern**. Select **Review** to turn the suggested entries into an editable batch. Check every date, activity, duration, site, and supervisor before saving.

This suggestion appears only when FieldX can identify a pattern in your prior entries. If it is absent, use **Manual Entry** or **Log With AI**.

## If your supervisor isn't in the dropdown

For a site-supervised activity, select a site first. The supervisor dropdown then lists supervisors linked to that site. You must choose a supervisor before you can save the entry.

### Add them from My Sites

1. Open the **My Sites** tab
2. On the site card, click **Add** next to **My Supervisor**
3. Pick an existing supervisor or invite them by name and email

That's the same invite as **Add Supervisor** on Timesheet — it just lives on the site card.

### Or use Timesheet Actions

Click **Actions** on the timesheet toolbar, choose **Add Supervisor**, and follow the form to add them to the correct site.

### If Add Supervisor is missing

Some programs hide **Add Supervisor** (and the **Add** button on My Sites). **Switch Supervisor** stays in **Actions** — it only works for people already linked to you.

To invite someone new, go to **Clearance Tasks**. If you're already approved, click **Update Application**, then on **Add Supervisors** click **Add new** and **Invite Site Supervisor**. Details: [Managing Site Information](/fieldx/students/step-5-my-sites#adding-a-supervisor-to-a-site) and [Getting Started & Clearance Tasks](/fieldx/students/step-1-getting-started-and-clearance-tasks#updating-an-approved-application).

## Logging hours with AI

For ad-hoc batch entry, the **Log With AI** option lets you paste a paragraph describing what you did (e.g., from your own notes) — the AI parses it into one or more timesheet entries you can review and confirm before saving.

## The timesheet table

Use **Table** and **Calendar** to switch between the entry list and a calendar. In the table:

- Use search and **Filters** to narrow the entries you see.
- Open a row's three-dot menu to select **See notes**, **Edit**, **Duplicate**, or **Delete** an entry. Availability depends on its approval status.
- Use **Actions** for supervisor tools and **Create personal report**.

The calendar lets you browse your schedule by week or month. Open an entry to inspect it. When logging is available, selecting time on the calendar can pre-fill a new entry.

### Personal reports

**Actions → Create personal report** creates a report for your supervisor to sign. Read the explanation in the dialog before continuing: personal reports are independent of your program's assigned reports and do not count toward cohort goals. Use the reports assigned in **Cohorts** for your program's normal approval process.

To download an analysis of your own hours, use the separate [Run Reports](/fieldx/students/step-7-running-reports) tab.

## Approved vs. unapproved

Each entry has one of two statuses:

- **Unapproved** — logged but not yet signed off on by your supervisor
- **Approved** — the required approval process for the entry is complete

Approval requires all signatures configured for the report, which can include your site supervisor and faculty. Your program also determines which activities count toward each requirement. A Program Admin can reopen a report for corrections; affected hours must complete approval again.

## Hour totals

At the top of the table you'll see hours totals by category for the selected period. The categories match your program's track requirements (e.g., Direct, Indirect, Supervision, Total).

### Switching between terms

If you've been in field experience for more than one term, a **Viewing:** dropdown appears above the totals bar. Use it to scope the hours summary — and the **Hours Details** modal — to a specific term.

- **All terms** — combined totals across every placement you've had
- **A specific term** (for example, _Fall 2025 · Practicum_) — totals, requirements, and progress for just that placement

Each term in the dropdown is tagged with its status:

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The term is currently in progress |
| **Upcoming** | The term hasn't started yet |
| **Past** | The term has ended |

When you open the Timesheet tab, the dropdown defaults to your current active term. Switching terms updates the totals bar, the progress numbers in **Hours Details**, and the requirements you're being measured against — useful when you move from Practicum to Internship and want to compare progress across levels.

<Note>
  The term selector only affects the summary bar and Hours Details modal. The timesheet table below still shows entries based on its own date and filter controls.
</Note>

### If your program pools a level series

Some programs combine a level series — for example, Internship I / II / III — into **one shared hour requirement**. You log hours in whichever term you're in, and only the combined total matters. If your track is set up this way:

- The **Viewing:** dropdown shows a **Combined** option in place of **All terms**, alongside each of your terms. Combined shows your progress across every term in the series against the single shared requirement.
- When you view one term of the series on its own, the totals show what you logged that term without a per-term goalpost. Your requirement applies to the series as a whole, not to any single term.
- In the **Hours Details** modal, the series appears as one entry in the **Level** dropdown, and a **Scope** dropdown switches between **Combined** and individual terms. The activity minimums shown there are pooled across the series.

## Editing & deleting entries

Open an entry's three-dot menu and select **Edit** or **Delete**. Use **Duplicate** to start a separate entry with similar details. Approved entries are locked against editing and deletion until a Program Admin unlocks them. Review your changes before selecting **Save Changes**.

<Note>
  See also: [Cohorts, Forms & Reports](/fieldx/students/step-4-the-cohorts-tab) for how cohort tasks and your learning contract work.
</Note>

## Common issues

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Supervisor isn't in the Log Hours dropdown | Add them from **My Sites** → site card → **Add**, or Timesheet **Actions** → **Add Supervisor**. |
| There's no Add Supervisor in the Actions menu | Your program hid student supervisor invites on Timesheet and My Sites. Go to **Clearance Tasks** (use **Update Application** if you're already approved) → **Add Supervisors** → **Add new**. |
| I need a different supervisor on future hours | Timesheet **Actions** → **Switch Supervisor**. That only lists people already linked to you. |
