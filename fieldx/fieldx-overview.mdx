## Not sure which role you are?

Hover over the left-hand side of your screen to open the sidebar. Your role is listed under your name in the top left.

Use this mapping to translate the label into your FieldX role:

| Label under your name | Your FieldX role |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** or **Student** | Student (Free or Student depends on whether you're paying for the simulation tool) |
| **Teacher** | Faculty Supervisor |
| **Supervisor** | Site Supervisor |
| **Admin** | Program Admin |

## What is FieldX?

FieldX (also called Field Experience) is SimCare's field-experience management module. It handles the full lifecycle of practicum and internship training, including clearance, hour logging, site and supervisor management, cohort organization, forms, reports, and program-wide compliance.
