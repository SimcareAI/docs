As a Faculty Supervisor, your students are organized into **cohorts** — groups tied to a specific course or placement term (for example, COU 690-002 or Spring Practicum). Joining the right cohort is the first thing you need to do in FieldX: it connects you to your students, their assignments, and their submitted documents so you can review and sign off on their work.

You only need to join a cohort once. After that, your students and their tasks will appear automatically in your Overview and Cohorts tabs.

## Two ways to join a cohort

### Invitation

The Program Admin at your students' institution can invite you directly by email. When they do:

1. You'll receive an email from [**hello@mail.simcare.ai**](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai) with a join link.
2. If you don't have a FieldX account yet, the link will walk you through creating one.
3. If you already have an account, clicking the link will add you to the cohort automatically — no extra steps needed.

<Note>
  Check your spam or junk folder if you don't see the invitation within a few minutes. Emails come from [hello@mail.simcare.ai](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai).
</Note>

### Cohort code

If your program gives you an 8-character cohort code instead of a direct invitation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to FieldX">
    Go to your institution's FieldX URL and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Cohorts tab">
    Navigate to the **Cohorts** tab in the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Join Cohort">
    Click the **Join Cohort** button in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    The **Join a Cohort as Instructor** modal opens. Type in the 8-character cohort code provided by your Program Admin and click **Join Course**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Enter your instructor join code, then select Join Course.">
  ![Join a Cohort as Instructor dialog with red arrows pointing to Join Code and Join Course](/images/fieldx/audit/faculty-join-cohort.png)
</Frame>

Once joined, the cohort appears in your list. Select **Open Cohort**, then **My Tasks**, to review tasks for that cohort.

<Note>
  Once inside a cohort, you'll see your specific group of students and any assignments related to them.
</Note>

## Creating your own cohort

Some programs let faculty supervisors create cohorts directly. If this applies to you, click **Create Cohort** on the Cohorts tab and enter a track code, name, and optional description.

If you don't see a **Create Cohort** button, your program manages cohort creation centrally — contact your Program Admin.

## Common issues

**I didn't receive an invitation email.** Check your spam or junk folder. Emails come from [hello@mail.simcare.ai](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai). If it's not there, ask your Program Coordinator to resend the invitation or provide you with a cohort code instead.

**The invitation link says it has expired or is invalid.** Invitation links can expire. Contact your Program Coordinator and ask them to send a new invitation, or use the cohort code method to join directly.

**I joined a cohort but I don't see any students.** Check that students are enrolled in the cohort and clear any search or term filters. If a student is enrolled but still missing, contact your Program Coordinator.

**I entered a cohort code but got an error.** Double-check the code with your Program Coordinator — codes are case-sensitive and may contain zeros vs. the letter O. Make sure there are no extra spaces when pasting.

**I can see the cohort but it shows the wrong students.** You may have joined the wrong cohort. Contact your Program Coordinator to confirm which cohort code corresponds to your group of students.
