Before students begin using FieldX, test the setup from their perspective and complete a final Program Admin QA pass.

## Use the right testing method

FieldX has two complementary testing methods:

| Method | Best for | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **View as Student** | Checking navigation, clearance instructions, track and term choices, visible sites, assigned work, and locked or unlocked states | Strictly read-only: you cannot submit forms, log hours, sign, approve, or change student data |
| **Populated demo data** | Reviewing completed forms, multi-signer progress, hour graphs, generated reports, PDFs, and faculty or supervisor queues | Demo records may be staged or stale; confirm what is representative before relying on it |

<Warning>
  View as Student does **not** replace a populated demo environment for end-to-end workflow testing. It replaces the need to switch into a separate student login when you only need to inspect what a student can see.
</Warning>

## Start View as Student

Launch it from any of these locations:

1. **My Program → Members → Students** — open a student's actions menu and select **View as Student**
2. **Cohorts → cohort → Members** — open a student's actions menu and select **View as Student**
3. **Student Details** — select **View as Student** at the top of the modal

An amber banner shows the student's name, the remaining session time, and **Exit Student View**. The session is audit-logged and expires automatically after 30 minutes.

## Required pre-launch test sequence

### 1. Check the clearance experience

Confirm that the student sees:

- The correct program, level, and track names
- The correct academic term choices
- Clear instructions for every clearance step
- Only the agreements and requirements that apply to that student
- The correct site-selection and supervisor-contact behavior
- A clear final-review and submission step

If a requirement is conditional, state the condition in both the step description and the form itself.

### 2. Check sites and supervisors

Verify that:

- Established sites are available and deduplicated
- Expired or inactive sites are not presented as active choices
- New-site requests follow the intended approval rule
- The supervisor instructions name the correct email sender: **SimCare**
- The person authorized to sign an agreement is not being automatically replaced by the site supervisor

### 3. Check forms and assignments

Open every required form and confirm:

- The correct person completes every question
- Student, site supervisor, faculty, and custom signer roles are correctly labeled
- Required signatures appear in the intended sequence
- Instructions, due dates, and assignment names are understandable
- No duplicated or orphaned questions remain

View as Student lets you inspect the form, but it will not let you submit it. Use populated demo data or a controlled test account when you must verify a completed multi-signer workflow.

### 4. Check hours and recurring schedules

Confirm the student's dashboard shows the correct:

- Total, direct, indirect, and supervision requirements
- Activity types and qualifying-hour rules
- Hours report template
- Site and supervisor choices
- First recurring assignment date, frequency, due date, and schedule end date

A recurring schedule is a generator. If it has not reached its first run date, look under **Schedules**, not completed assignments.

### 5. Check the populated outputs

Using populated demo data, verify:

- Overview KPI cards and progress graphs
- A completed weekly hour report
- The generated PDF
- Multi-signer status and the person currently blocking completion
- Faculty and site supervisor task queues
- At least one standard report from **Run Report**

If a demo artifact is stale, say so clearly and identify what differs in the current version.

### 6. Check invitations and account access

Before launch:

- Confirm newly added members received credentials or know they must self-sign up
- Search for users accidentally added under the wrong role
- Confirm SSO or LMS access works for the school's domain
- Test one supervisor invitation and confirm the message arrives from **SimCare**
- Verify the institution and the FieldX program are both connected to the LMS, when applicable

## Faculty and supervisor perspectives

There is no dedicated View as Faculty mode. To test faculty workflows, use a faculty account or join a controlled cohort as faculty.

For site supervisors, use [View as Supervisor](/fieldx/view-as-supervisor). To test the invitation and first-login experience itself, use a controlled supervisor email and complete the supervisor step from a controlled student account.

## Final launch decision

Do not launch based only on whether the pages open. The program is ready when its terms, tracks, hour rules, sites, forms, signature routes, recurring schedules, invitations, and approval flow have all been checked.

Use the [Launch Readiness & QA Checklist](/fieldx/launch-readiness-qa) for the full sign-off list.
