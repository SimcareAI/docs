Site supervisors interact with FieldX through invitation links, task emails, their dashboard, and profile requirements. Test the entire path before launch because supervisor access problems block student reports and agreements.

## How supervisors are invited

A supervisor can be added:

- When a student enters the supervisor during clearance
- By a Program Admin from a site
- Through a controlled onboarding workflow

The invitation email comes from **SimCare**, not FieldX. Ask supervisors to search for “SimCare” and check spam or quarantine.

A supervisor link creates or opens the account and connects the person to the relevant site and student workflow. Resent links may replace earlier links.

## Before inviting supervisors

Confirm:

- Correct email address
- Correct site
- Correct student relationship
- Correct signer role
- Whether the supervisor already has an account
- Whether profile documents are required
- Who should sign agreements when it is not the site supervisor

Do not use a student's best guess at an email when the program can verify the supervisor contact first.

## Profile requirements

Programs may require supervisors to provide items such as:

- Liability insurance
- Professional license
- Supervision training
- Credentials or certifications
- Other program-specific documents

Required fields can block the supervisor from completing a task until submitted. Optional fields can usually be dismissed.

Profile requirements belong to the supervisor's account, so a supervisor generally completes them once rather than once per student.

<Warning>
  Adding a new required profile field can affect supervisors who are already active. Tell them before the change so the next-login prompt is expected.
</Warning>

Program Admins can review uploaded information from the site's supervisor list and profile view. Uploading a required item does not necessarily imply a separate Program Admin approval step; verify the configured workflow before promising review.

## Test the invitation flow

Use a controlled email address:

1. Add the supervisor to a test site or controlled student placement.
2. Confirm the invitation arrives from SimCare.
3. Open the link.
4. Complete or inspect the profile prompt.
5. Confirm the correct site and student appear.
6. Confirm the expected report or form is visible.
7. Check the signer order.
8. Exit without completing a live student's work.

Use [View as Supervisor](/fieldx/view-as-supervisor) when you need a safe, read-only view of an existing supervisor's dashboard.

## Missing email checklist

If a supervisor cannot find the invitation:

1. Confirm the email spelling.
2. Search for “SimCare.”
3. Check spam, quarantine, and institutional email filtering.
4. Confirm an invitation was generated.
5. Check whether the supervisor already used an earlier link.
6. Resend the invitation after any cooldown.
7. If needed, use the standard password-reset route for an existing account.

## Missing signature checklist

If a supervisor or final signer reports no task:

1. Open the form's **Submissions** view.
2. Confirm the person is actually in the signer list.
3. Identify the signer currently blocking the sequential workflow.
4. Confirm the student reached that step.
5. Check the person's FieldX account, not only email.
6. Verify the form was assigned to the correct cohort and student.

## Ending a supervisor relationship

Removing a supervisor from a site preserves historical reports and signatures. Before removal:

- Review unsigned work
- Reassign responsibility where needed
- Confirm affected students have another supervisor
- Record the effective end of the placement

## What to send supervisors

Provide a one-page guide containing:

- The SimCare sender name
- How to create or access the account
- What emails they will receive
- How to sign hours and evaluations
- Required profile documents
- Support contact
- A screenshot or short video when possible
