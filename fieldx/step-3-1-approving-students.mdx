title: "Step 3.1: Approving students"
description: "How to review and approve student clearance tasks before they can begin logging hours."
---

Once a student completes all their required **Clearance Tasks** (liability forms, site selection, supervisor contacts, etc.), their application appears in your approval queue.

## Finding pending approvals

A few ways to find students waiting for approval:

1. **Overview tab → Pending Tasks KPI card** — click to filter the student table
2. **Overview tab → student row** — students with a pending clearance status are flagged in the table
3. **Activity Log** (My Program → Activity Log) — filter by **Type: Application Forms**

You can also filter the Overview student table by **Application Status** (under **More filters**) to see every student in a specific stage — App: Not Started, App: Started, App: Pending Approval, App: Approved, or App: Rejected.

## Reviewing a student's submission

Click any student to open their **Student Details** modal, then go to the **Documents** tab. The full pre-application appears with every field they entered and every document they uploaded during clearance, including:

- Track selection
- Signed agreements
- Selected sites with supervisor contacts
- Liability insurance upload
- Any custom questions, forms, or quizzes you added

## Taking action

If the application is in `Pending` status, the Documents tab shows two buttons:

- **Approve** — grants the student full access to begin logging hours and participating in their assigned cohorts
- **Reject** — sends the application back. You'll be prompted to leave a note explaining what needs to be fixed

If the application has already been approved, a **Revert to Rejected** action becomes available — useful if you need to put a student back into clearance after a credential expires.

<Note>The student sees your specific feedback the next time they log in, so they know exactly what to correct before re-submitting.</Note>

### Approving an updated application

Students can also update their own approved application from the **Clearance Tasks** page (for example, to add a new site or fix a detail). When that happens, a new pending submission from that student appears in your queue while their previous approval stays active — the student keeps full Field Experience access until you review the update. Review it the same way as a first-time application; on approval, the update replaces the previously approved version.
## Granting a term extension

If your program uses [submission windows](/fieldx/step-0-2-customization-settings#submission-windows-optional) to close new applications after a term's cutoff date, you can grant a specific student an extension so they can still select or continue that term — no link or token, they just log in normally.

You can start a grant from three places:

- **Clearance & Placement tab → Grant Extension** — pick the term, the student, and a deadline.
- **My Program → Terms and Tracks → term row → Extensions** — pre-fills the term; pick the student and deadline.
- **My Program → Members → student row → ⋮ → Manage Clearance** — pre-fills the student; pick the term and deadline.

Set an **Extended Until** date — the student can select or continue that term through end of day on that date. Only one active extension can exist per student per term; granting a new one replaces the old.

To end an extension early, open the same modal, switch to the **Past** list, and revoke it. Revoked and expired extensions stay in the list as an audit trail.

Extensions never affect students already placed in the term — they only unlock term selection during a new pre-application.

## Managing submission windows in bulk

To adjust several terms' open and close dates without editing each term individually, open the **Clearance & Placement** tab and click **Manage Submission Windows** in the header. Every active term appears with editable date fields and a **Save** button per row. Both buttons only appear when **Restrict Applications by Submission Window** is enabled under **My Program → Settings**.

## Bulk handling

For programs with large cohorts coming through clearance at the same time:

- Use the **Members** tab in My Program to sort students by clearance status
- Group students by track or term and review them in batches
- The Activity Log gives you a sortable, exportable history of every approval decision
