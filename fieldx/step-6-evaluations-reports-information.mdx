## Choose a report

### Hours

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Hours Summary** | Hours per student and activity type, with optional breakdowns by week, supervisor, site, or cohort |
| **Hours Log** | Individual hour entries, including their status and approval details |
| **Hours Trend Chart** | Weekly and cumulative hours with activity breakdowns |

**Hours Summary** combines the former **Weekly Activity Ledger**, **Multi-Week Hours Progress**, and **Hours Summary Snapshot**. **Hours Log** replaces **Complete Hours Entry Log**. You can search the catalog using a former name.

### Supervision and requirements

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Supervision Summary** | Supervision hours by form and provider, including weekly averages |
| **Weekly Supervision Check** | Weekly supervision gaps against thresholds or a requirement set; formerly Weekly Supervision Compliance Audit |
| **Requirement Check** | Which configured requirements each student meets, remaining hours, and optional pending-hour impact |

For **Requirement Check**, select an existing requirement set. Use **Manage requirement sets** in the catalog when you need to configure one first.

### Forms and evaluations

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Assignment Completion Status** | Complete, pending, and overdue assignments, including the role still needed; formerly Assignment Completion Ledger |
| **Form Export** | Selected form responses in CSV or Excel |
| **Student Competency Profile** | A narrative based on competency evaluation results for a student, cohort, or term |
| **Site Quality Assurance Report** | A narrative based on your program's site evaluation results |

### Sites, supervisors, and system exports

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Supervisor Credentials Roster** | Supervisor qualifications, missing items, and expiration status; formerly Site & Preceptor Credentialing Report |
| **Clearance Funnel Velocity** | Applications waiting in clearance and the time to submit or approve them |
| **Incomplete Pre-Applications** | Students who started an application but have not submitted it |
| **Application Site & Supervisor Export** | Site and supervisor details from student applications; availability depends on your institution |
| **Activity Log Export (System Audit Trail)** | Raw activity records for program audits |

Your program may also show specialized reports, such as **BBS Weekly Log** and **Practicum Certification Summary**, when the corresponding configuration is enabled. The catalog shows the reports available to your role and program.

## Configure and run a report

1. Click **Preview** on a catalog card to read what the report includes, or **Run report** to configure it.
2. Choose the **Population** when available: terms, cohorts, sites, supervisors, or individual students.
3. Set the reporting **Period** and any **Experience type** or **Approval status** filters.
4. Review the report-specific options, such as **Break down by**, supervision thresholds, or a requirement set.
5. Choose an **Output** format. Most tabular reports support **Excel**, **CSV**, and **PDF**; individual reports may offer fewer formats.
6. Click **Preview first 200 rows** to inspect a sample, or generate the full file using the main action.

The preview is a sample. Use **Generate full** with the selected format to download the complete result. Large reports can run in the background and appear under **Recent reports** when ready.

Some reports, including **Hours Trend Chart** and **Form Export**, have their own configuration screen and result view. Follow the options shown for that report.

## Save settings for next time

In the shared configuration screen, click **Save this configuration**, enter a name, and save it. Saved configurations appear in the report catalog so you can run the same selection again. **Recent reports** also lets you download or rerun previous jobs.

## Ask AI for reports

You can also ask [Ask AI](/fieldx/coordinator-ask-ai) to run a report by name, summarize a table, build a chart, or prepare a custom Word report. Include the term, cohort, date range, and output format in your request.

For a report your program needs that is not covered here, contact [tiko@simcare.ai](mailto:tiko@simcare.ai) with the required data and format.
