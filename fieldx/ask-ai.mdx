Open **Ask AI** in your FieldX navigation to ask about students, cohorts, sites, hours, clearance, and assignments. Program Admins can also draft administrative changes and review them before applying them.

## Start a conversation

1. Open the **Ask AI** tab.
2. Select a suggested prompt or type your question in the message box.
3. Include the term, cohort, student, and date range that matter to your question.
4. Review the answer and follow up to narrow it down or change the format.

Click **What can I ask?** to browse the available questions, reports, spreadsheet tools, and actions.

## Things to try

- "Which students have assigned forms that are still pending, and what are the due dates?"
- "List all cohorts with their term, faculty supervisor, and number of students."
- "Which active sites have affiliation agreements expiring in the next 90 days?"
- "Run the Hours Summary for the current term, broken down by cohort."
- "Chart total approved hours by cohort as a bar chart."
- "How do I invite a site supervisor?"

Table results support filtering, sorting, copying, and **Export CSV**. Built-in reports provide the download formats supported by that report, including Excel, CSV, or PDF.

## Reports, files, and admin actions

You can attach Excel or CSV files through the **+** menu and ask Ask AI to summarize or compare them with your program data. The same menu includes **Generate Report** for a custom Word report.

For actions such as inviting members, creating cohorts, or assigning forms, Ask AI prepares a review card. Check the people, dates, roles, and proposed changes before clicking **Approve**.

See the [full Program Admin Ask AI guide](/fieldx/coordinator-ask-ai) for report exports, attachments, saved conversations, and approval steps.
