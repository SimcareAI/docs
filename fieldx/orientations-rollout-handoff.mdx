A successful configuration is not the same as a successful rollout. Plan the handoff so Program Admins, faculty, students, and site supervisors know what to do before the first live assignment.

## Standard rollout sequence

1. Environment configuration
2. Program Admin walkthrough
3. Client exploration and corrections
4. Faculty orientation
5. Student orientation
6. Go-live
7. First post-launch check-in

Faculty orientation should happen before student orientation because faculty need time to understand their queues, signer responsibilities, and support role.

## Faculty orientation

Schedule one to four weeks before go-live when possible.

Cover:

- Accessing FieldX
- Joining or managing cohorts
- Viewing student progress
- Finding pending work
- Signing reports and evaluations
- What supervisor and student emails look like
- How to get support
- Copy-from-existing and recurring-schedule rules for future terms

Invite site supervisors when the signing and dashboard material will help them. Record the session even when attendance is small.

## Student orientation

Schedule during the week before go-live or the week of launch.

Cover:

- Signing in
- Completing clearance
- Choosing a site and adding a supervisor
- Responding to rejected clearance
- Joining the correct cohort
- Logging hours
- Completing and signing reports
- Finding assignments and due dates
- Getting support

Keep the live demonstration focused. The goal is to make students confident enough to begin, not to cover every advanced feature.

## Format choices

Orientations can be:

- Live and optional, with a recording
- Built into an existing required program meeting
- Repeated for different populations or time zones
- Replaced by a recorded walkthrough when the workflow is simple
- Split between practicum and internship populations

Book 40–45 minutes even when the core demonstration takes 20–25 minutes so participants have time for questions.

## Handoff package

Send:

- Program Admin credentials
- Walkthrough recording
- Faculty orientation recording
- Student orientation recording
- Supervisor instructions
- School-specific support page
- Office-hours information
- Documentation links
- Primary SimCare contact
- Escalation route for commercial questions
- Known limitations or follow-ups promised during setup

Do not send credentials until corrections promised “before credentials” are complete.

## Rollout options

Programs do not need to launch every population at once.

Common approaches:

- Full launch at term start
- Pilot with one small cohort
- Phased launch by level
- Phased launch by term
- Program Admin preloads the first cohort during a mid-year migration

Choose the smallest rollout that tests the real workflow without creating unnecessary duplicate work.

## Before go-live

Teach these three rules explicitly:

1. **Copy from existing** is available only during cohort creation.
2. Hour-log schedules must be verified and recreated for the new term.
3. LMS cohort linking should be configured before faculty manually build sections.

## Schedule the next check-in

Book the first support call near a real pressure point:

- First clearance deadline
- First weekly report
- First supervisor sign-off
- First term boundary
- First LMS-linked course launch

Use the [Launch Readiness & QA Checklist](/fieldx/launch-readiness-qa) before sending the final handoff.
