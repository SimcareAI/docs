Your **Overview** tab is your home base. It shows all students across your placement site(s) and surfaces exactly what needs your attention so you do not have to hunt for it.

## KPI cards

At the top of the Overview tab, three summary cards give you a quick health check at a glance:

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Documents** | All reports and forms tied to your students, across all statuses |
| **Pending Signatures** | Items in Pending or In Progress that still need action |
| **Completed Documents** | Items recorded as complete for your role |

These numbers update in real time as students submit work and signatures are collected.

## Student table

Below the KPI cards is a table listing the students connected to your reports and forms. The table sorts automatically — students with the most pending tasks appear at the top so your highest-priority work is always visible first.

Each row shows:

- Student name and program
- Site name
- **N Tasks** button — click it to jump directly to the Tasks tab pre-filtered for that student

Use the search box above the table to filter by student name, track, or site name. This is especially useful if you supervise students at multiple sites.

## Tasks tab: where you take action

The Tasks tab is where you sign reports and complete forms. It has four sub-tabs:

| Sub-tab | What appears here |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Items submitted by a student, waiting for your review and signature |
| **In Progress** | Items with work started but still incomplete, such as an unfinished form |
| **Completed** | Items complete for your role; finalized documents can be downloaded from the report panel |
| **Rejected** | Items you have denied |

Use **All Students** and **All Types** to filter the task list, or search by task or student.

<Tip>
  If you supervise students at multiple sites, all of their tasks appear aggregated in one Tasks tab. Use the student name filter to focus on one person at a time.
</Tip>

## Viewing a student's hour logs

Before signing a report, you can review the individual timesheet entries that make it up. This is especially important for catching data entry errors before they become part of the official record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Tasks tab">
    Click **Tasks** in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Click any report in the Pending sub-tab. Reports open in a dialog. Forms open in a new browser tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click View Logs">
    Inside the report panel, click **View Logs**. You will see each individual time entry with its date, activity type, site, duration, and any notes the student added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review flagged entries">
    Your program can flag long entries in amber. The default threshold is 2 hours or more, but your program can change or disable it. Read the notice above the logs and confirm unexpected durations with the student.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The **Sign & Approve** button will be greyed out if the report has zero hours in its date range. Click **View Logs** to check the entries included in the report. If hours are missing, ask the student to verify the dates, site, supervisor, and their hour entries before you sign.
</Warning>

## Denying a report

If something is incorrect and you cannot approve the report:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a reason">
    Explain what needs to change in **Comments**. Choose **Everyone** if the student should see the reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Deny">
    Click **Deny** in the report panel to open the confirmation dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Deny** in the dialog. The report moves to **Rejected**. Faculty or a Program Admin can arrange a resubmission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When writing a denial comment, you can set visibility to **Everyone** (student can see it) or **Hidden from students** (internal note only). Comments support threaded replies, so you can have a back-and-forth conversation directly on the document.

## Students tab: browsing a student's full timesheet

The **Students** tab gives you a read-only view of every hour entry a student has logged with you — not just the entries attached to one report. Use it to answer questions like "how many hours has this student logged with me overall?" without opening individual reports.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Students tab">
    Click **Students** in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a student">
    Use the student dropdown at the top right of the table. Only students you actively supervise appear in the list. The first student loads automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review their entries">
    Each row shows the activity, duration, site, supervisor, and date and time. Use the row's available note action to read any notes the student added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Choose a student, then switch to Calendar to review their entries by date. This example uses an administrator's read-only supervisor view.">
  ![Supervisor Students timesheet with red arrows pointing to the student selector and Calendar, with an administrator read-only banner](/images/fieldx/audit/supervisor-students.png)
</Frame>

You can switch between two views:

- **Table** — a sortable, paginated list. Filter by activity type, site, track, date range, or approval status, or search across entries.
- **Calendar** — the same entries plotted on a month or week calendar, matching the calendar view students use on their own Timesheet tab.

<Note>
  The Students tab only shows hours the student logged **with you**. Entries the student logged under a different supervisor do not appear. The view is also read-only — you cannot add, edit, approve, or delete entries here. Approving hours still happens through report signing on the Tasks tab.
</Note>

## My Site tab

The **My Site** tab shows a card for each placement site you supervise. Each card displays:

- Site name, address, and logo
- A roster of active students placed there

From each card you can upload a site logo, edit site details, or remove yourself from the site.

<Note>
  If your My Site tab shows "No sites yet," it means no students have added you as their supervisor yet. A site appears here once at least one student selects you as their supervisor during their placement setup.
</Note>

## Email notifications and weekly digest

You do not need to log in to stay on top of your queue:

- **Email notification** — when a student submits a report or form, you receive an email from `hello@mail.simcare.ai`. You can sign the document directly from the email link without logging into the dashboard.
- **Weekly digest** — a summary of outstanding signatures helps you plan your reviews. Use it as a weekly checklist.

<Tip>
  If you prefer to batch your signing, use the weekly digest as a prompt. If you prefer to sign as items come in, the individual submission emails make that easy too.
</Tip>

## Common issues

**Sign & Approve is greyed out and I cannot click it.** The report has zero hours in its date range. Click **View Logs** and ask the student to verify the dates, site, supervisor, and hour entries. Refresh after corrections; contact your Program Admin if the total still looks wrong.

**I am not receiving email notifications.** Check your spam or junk folder. All emails come from `hello@mail.simcare.ai` — add this address to your contacts or safe-senders list so future messages are not filtered.

**I cannot find a specific student.** Use the search box on the Overview tab or the Tasks tab. Type the student's name, their program track, or the site name. If the student still does not appear, they may not have added you as their supervisor yet — ask them to confirm your name is listed on their placement.

**My Site tab is empty.** No students have selected you as their supervisor yet. Once a student adds you during their placement setup, your site will appear automatically.

**I accidentally denied a report.** Contact the student's faculty supervisor or Program Admin to arrange a resubmission. You cannot undo a denial yourself from the dashboard.

**A student's task count says 0 but I know they submitted something.** Try refreshing the page. If the task still does not appear, ask the student to confirm they clicked **Submit** and not just **Save**. Drafts saved by the student are not sent to you until they explicitly submit.
