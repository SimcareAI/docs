As a Site Supervisor, your role is focused on supporting the student(s) placed at your site. You don't need to manage cohorts or classes — your dashboard gives you exactly what you need to review students and sign off on their hours.

## Getting your account

You'll receive an email invitation when:

- A student requests to be placed at your site and lists you as their supervisor, or
- A Program Admin manually adds you to the program

For a new account, look for **Welcome to SimCare - You've Been Added as a Supervisor** from **SimCare Field Experience** at [hello@mail.simcare.ai](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai). The email includes your sign-in URL, email address, and a temporary password. Select **Sign In to SimCare** and use those details.

Your account has already been created. If the temporary password does not work, select **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page. If you already use SimCare, sign in with your existing account and ask your Program Admin to confirm the supervisor assignment.

<Warning>
  Check your spam or junk folder if you don't see the invitation within a few minutes. Add [hello@mail.simcare.ai](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai) to your contacts to ensure future notifications land in your inbox.
</Warning>

## First login: complete your profile

On your first login, you may see a prompt to complete your professional profile. Open **My Profile** to add:

- Your CV
- License type
- Whether you have completed supervision training (yes/no)
- Years of licensed practice

<Note>
  Programs use these details for accreditation. The optional prompt has **Not Now** and **Go to My Profile** buttons. If your program requires completion, choose **Complete My Profile** and fill in its outstanding items before using the other tabs. See [My Profile](/fieldx/site/step-4-my-profile).
</Note>

## Your dashboard

Once activated, your supervisor dashboard has five tabs:

| Tab | What's here |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | KPI cards (Total Documents, Pending Signatures, Completed Documents) \+ your student list, sorted by most pending tasks first |
| **Tasks** | All reports and forms awaiting your action, with Sign & Approve, Deny, and View Logs |
| **Students** | A read-only timesheet of the hours each student has logged with you, in table or calendar view |
| **My Site** | Cards for each site you supervise, with student rosters and site details |
| **My Profile** | Your professional credentials (CV, license type, supervision training, years licensed) |

### Overview tab

The Overview tab is your home base. The three KPI cards at the top give you a quick count of where things stand across all your students. Below that, your student list is sorted so students with the most pending tasks appear first — so you always know who needs attention.

### Tasks tab

The Tasks tab is where you take action on submitted reports and forms. It has four sub-tabs:

| Sub-tab | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Awaiting your signature |
| **In Progress** | Items with work started but still incomplete, such as an unfinished form |
| **Completed** | Items complete for your role; check the report panel for any remaining signatures |
| **Rejected** | You denied the submission |

Use **All Students** to choose a student and **All Types** to choose forms or reports. You can also search the task list. If you supervise students at multiple sites, all of their tasks appear here in one aggregated view.

<Note>
  **Sign & Approve** is greyed out if the report has zero hours in its date range. Click **View Logs** to check the entries included in that report. Ask the student to verify the dates, site, supervisor, and hours if the total is zero.
</Note>

**View Logs** opens a detailed breakdown of each hour entry on the report: date, type, site, duration, and notes. Your program can flag long entries in amber for review. The default threshold is 2 hours or more; your program can change or disable it.

**Deny flow:** Add your reason in **Comments**, then click **Deny** and confirm in the dialog. The submission moves to **Rejected**. Faculty or a Program Admin can arrange a resubmission.

**Comments:** Choose **Everyone** for a comment the student should see, or **Hidden from students** for an internal note visible to supervisors, faculty, and Program Admins. Comments support threaded replies.

### Students tab

The Students tab shows every hour entry a student has logged with you, across all reports. Pick a student from the dropdown to see their full timesheet in table or calendar view. The view is read-only — approving or denying hours still happens through report signing on the Tasks tab. See [Viewing Student Progress](/fieldx/site/step-2-viewing-student-progress) for details.

### My Site tab

My Site shows a card for each site you supervise, including the site logo, address, and student roster. From here you can upload a site logo, edit site details, or remove yourself from a site.

<Note>
  If the My Site tab appears empty, no students have added you as their supervisor yet. Once a student lists you, their site will appear here automatically.
</Note>

### My Profile tab

Your professional profile — CV, license type, supervision training completion, and years of licensed practice. This information is used for program accreditation reporting and is collected once.

## Approving without logging in

You don't always need to log into the dashboard to sign off. When a student submits a report, you'll receive an email notification with a direct link to review and sign — no login required. You'll also receive a **weekly digest** listing outstanding signatures.

<Tip>
  Next step: Once your account is active, you'll see your students and any pending tasks on the Overview. See [Viewing Student Progress](/fieldx/site/step-2-viewing-student-progress).
</Tip>

## Common issues

**Sign & Approve is greyed out**

The report has zero hours in its date range. Click **View Logs** and ask the student to verify the report dates, site, supervisor, and hour entries. Refresh after corrections; contact your Program Admin if the report total still looks wrong.

**I'm not receiving invitation or notification emails**

Check your spam or junk folder. All emails come from [hello@mail.simcare.ai](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai). If you find them in spam, mark as "not spam" and add the address to your contacts so future emails come through reliably.

**I can't find a specific student**

Use the search field on the Tasks tab or the Overview tab to filter by student name. If the student doesn't appear at all, they may not have added you as their supervisor yet — ask them to confirm your name is listed in their placement details.

**The profile setup modal keeps appearing every time I log in**

The optional first-login prompt is suppressed after you dismiss it or save profile information. A program-required prompt is separate: open **My Profile**, complete the listed outstanding items, and click **Save Profile** to unlock the portal.

**My Site tab is empty**

No students have listed you as their supervisor yet. Once a student adds you in their placement request, your site card will appear automatically. No action needed on your end.

**A student's report is in "In Progress" but nothing is happening**

Reopen the item to check its saved response and signature status. Opening a signing window alone does not complete a signature. A report you have signed can move to **Completed** for your role while faculty signatures are still outstanding; check the report panel before treating the whole document as finalized.
