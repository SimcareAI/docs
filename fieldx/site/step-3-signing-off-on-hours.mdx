Signing student hours is done through the **Tasks** tab on your supervisor dashboard. When a student submits a supervision report, it lands in your task queue waiting for your e-signature — nothing is finalized until you sign off.

## How to sign a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tasks tab">
    Click the **Tasks** tab on your supervisor dashboard. You'll see all pending reports across every student you supervise. If you supervise students at multiple sites, reports from all sites appear here in one aggregated list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the pending report">
    Pending reports appear on the **Pending** status tab. Use **All Students** or the search box to find a student or report.

    Use **All Types** to show reports or assigned forms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the hours">
    Click the report row (or **Get Started**) to open the report panel. Click **View Logs** to see each individual timesheet entry — date, activity type, site, duration, and any notes the student added.

    Your program can flag long entries in amber. The default threshold is 2 hours or more; your program can change or disable it. Read the notice above the logs and confirm unexpected durations before signing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign & Approve">
    Click **Sign & Approve**. An embedded DocuSeal e-signature window opens — complete your part of the signature flow. After you sign, the report can move to **Completed** for your role. Check the signature status in the panel: the finalized PDF becomes available after all required parties have signed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The report's View Logs and Sign & Approve controls. This example is shown in administrator read-only mode; sign reports from your own supervisor account.">
  ![Supervisor hours report with red arrows pointing to View Logs and Sign and Approve, with an administrator read-only banner](/images/fieldx/audit/supervisor-hours-report.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  **Sign & Approve is disabled (greyed out) if a report has zero hours in its date range.** Click **View Logs** to check the included entries. If the total is zero, ask the student to verify the report dates, site, supervisor, and their hour entries. Refresh after corrections.
</Note>

## If you need to deny a report

If the hours look incorrect or the report needs revision, click **Deny** in the report panel. Before confirming, add your reason in the **Comments** section at the bottom of the panel — this message is visible to the student and their faculty supervisor so they understand what needs to change.

When you're ready, click **Deny** in the confirmation dialog to confirm.

A denied report moves to the **Rejected** tab. It is not deleted — faculty or a Program Admin can issue a resubmit flow if the student needs to correct and resubmit. You can still view the report read-only from the Rejected tab.

<Tip>
  When writing a denial reason, be specific. "Hours look incorrect on 3/14" is more actionable for the student than "Please review." The comment thread supports replies, so the student can follow up directly if they have questions.
</Tip>

### Comment visibility

When leaving comments, you can choose between **Everyone** (visible to the student, faculty, and Program Admins) or **Hidden from students** (visible only to supervisors and faculty). Use hidden comments for internal notes that aren't meant for the student.

## Status tab meanings

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Awaiting your signature |
| **In Progress** | Items with work started but still incomplete, such as an unfinished form |
| **Completed** | Items complete for your role; download the finalized PDF once all required parties have signed |
| **Rejected** | Denied — accessible read-only; can be resubmitted by faculty or Program Admin |

## Downloading or printing a completed form

Assigned forms open differently than reports: clicking **Fill Form** opens the form in a new browser tab at `/assigned-forms`. Once the form has a saved or submitted response, look for **Download PDF** on the form. Click **Download PDF** to open a printable PDF, then print from your browser or PDF viewer.

## Approval via email

When a student submits a report, you receive an email notification from [**hello@mail.simcare.ai**](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai). Open the signing link in that email to review the report and complete your signature. A direct signing link can open the signature flow without first visiting your dashboard.

<Tip>
  A weekly digest lists outstanding signatures across your students. Use it to catch up on approvals in one session without monitoring the dashboard daily.
</Tip>

## Common issues

**Sign & Approve is greyed out**

Click **View Logs** to check the report's included entries. Ask the student to verify the dates, site, supervisor, and hour entries if the total is zero. Refresh after corrections; contact your Program Admin if the total still looks wrong.

**I'm not receiving email notifications**

Check your spam or junk folder. Notification emails come from [**hello@mail.simcare.ai**](mailto:hello@mail.simcare.ai) — add this address to your contacts or safe senders list to ensure they arrive in your inbox.

**I can't find a specific student's report**

Use the search bar on the Tasks tab to search by student name. Make sure the **Pending** tab is selected — reports waiting for your signature won't appear on the Completed or Rejected tabs. If the student appears nowhere, they may not have submitted a report yet, or they may not have added you as their supervisor on their profile.

**A report I signed is no longer showing**

Check **Completed** for reports you have signed. Open the report to check any remaining signatures. Download the finalized document once all required parties have signed.

**I denied a report by mistake**

Contact the student's faculty supervisor or a program coordinator — they can issue a resubmit flow so the student can resubmit without losing their original hours. You cannot undo a denial yourself.

**The DocuSeal signature window isn't loading**

Try opening the report in a different browser or disabling browser extensions (ad blockers sometimes interfere). If the issue persists, contact your program coordinator.
