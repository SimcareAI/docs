The **My Profile** tab is where you maintain your professional credentials. Programs use this information for accreditation and quality-assurance compliance — it's not just a display profile.

## Opening My Profile

Click the **My Profile** tab in your supervisor navigation. The card heading reads **Professional Profile**.

## Profile fields

Add your professional information and complete the items required by your program:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **CV / Resume** | Upload a PDF, DOC, or DOCX file up to 10 MB. Drag and drop or click to browse. |
| **License Type** | Select all applicable credentials. The list includes counseling, social work, psychology, teaching, and other credentials. Choose **Other (specify)** and fill in **Specify License** if yours is not listed. |
| **Supervision training** | Yes/No — have you received formal training as a supervisor? Selecting Yes reveals an optional details field. |
| **Years of Licensed Practice** | Enter the number of years you have been licensed. |
| **Hide Email from Site** | Turn this on to keep your email out of the list students use to select a past site supervisor. |

Click **Save Profile** when done. The button briefly changes to **Saved**. You can save partial progress and return to complete outstanding requirements.

<Note>
  Program staff with profile access, including faculty and Program Admins, can review your CV and professional credentials. Your core credentials are shared profile information used across the programs you work with.
</Note>

## The profile prompt on first login

The first time you log in, a modal may appear prompting you to complete your profile. You can:

- Click **Go to My Profile** to navigate there immediately
- Click **Not Now** to dismiss and complete it later

The optional prompt is suppressed after you dismiss it or save profile information. This does not satisfy a program's required fields: a program can still block the portal until its outstanding requirements are complete.

## When your program requires profile completion

Some programs require supervisors to complete their profile before working in the portal. When that requirement applies to you, the supervisor portal locks down until you fill in what the program asked for.

You will see:

- A **Complete Your Professional Profile** modal that cannot be dismissed with **Not Now**. The header reads **Required by [program name]** (or **Required by N programs** if more than one program is waiting on you).
- A list of the programs holding you up and how many items each one is still waiting for (for example, `Anytown University — 3 outstanding`).
- **Overview**, **Tasks**, **Students**, and **My Site** disabled in the navigation. Only **My Profile** remains available.

Click **Complete My Profile** to close the required prompt and open the profile form. Fill in the requested items and click **Save Profile**. As soon as the outstanding items clear, the disabled tabs unlock and you can return to Overview, Tasks, or My Site without reloading.

<Note>Answers to program-specific questions are stored under the program that asked them and are available to its authorized staff. A program can retain those answers after you stop working with it. Your core credentials, such as your CV and licenses, are shared profile information.</Note>

Programs may also require answers beyond the core credential fields (for example, additional license numbers or program-specific questions). Those extra fields appear on the **My Profile** tab alongside the core fields and count toward the outstanding total shown in the blocking modal.

## Updating your profile

You can update your profile at any time — click **My Profile**, make changes, and save. There is no cooldown or approval required.

## Updating your name

Your supervisor legal/display name is part of your SimCare account, not the FieldX **My Profile** credential card. To update it, open **Account Settings**, stay on **Profile**, edit **First Name** and **Last Name**, then save.

Use the name that should appear on FieldX records and match your professional license. If you cannot access Account Settings or the name does not update after saving, contact [hello@simcare.ai](mailto:hello@simcare.ai) or use the support chat.

## Common issues

**The profile prompt keeps appearing after I saved.**<br />If the prompt names a program, check its outstanding requirements, including any program-specific questions. Saving partial information does not clear a required item that is still blank.

**I can't upload my CV — the file won't accept.**<br />Accepted formats are PDF, DOC, and DOCX. Files larger than 10 MB will be rejected. Try compressing your PDF or saving as a smaller format.

**I don't see a My Profile tab.**
My Profile is a supervisor-only tab. If you don't see it, confirm you're logged in as a Site Supervisor (not a student or faculty account). Contact [hello@simcare.ai](mailto:hello@simcare.ai) if your role seems incorrect.

**The Overview, Tasks, Students, and My Site tabs are grayed out.**
Your program requires profile completion before you can use the rest of the portal. Open **My Profile**, complete the items listed in the blocking modal, and save. The tabs unlock as soon as the outstanding items clear.
