Recurring schedules generate assignments automatically throughout a term. Use them for weekly hour logs, supervision reports, evaluations, and other work that repeats on a predictable cadence.

## Decide the policy before building the schedule

For every recurring report, confirm with the program:

- **Cadence** — weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or another supported interval
- **First assignment date**
- **Due date offset** — how many days the recipient has to complete it
- **Schedule end date**
- **Student signature** — required or not
- **Site supervisor signature** — required or not
- **Faculty signature** — required or not
- **Default signing faculty** — program-wide, cohort-specific, or student-specific

<Warning>
  Do not guess the signature cadence or signers. These decisions affect every student and are among the most common sources of post-launch corrections.
</Warning>

## Create a recurring schedule

1. Open **Assignments**.
2. Find the form or report template and select **Assign**.
3. Choose the target cohort or individuals.
4. Turn on **Repeat**.
5. Set the start date.
6. Choose the frequency.
7. Set the due date offset.
8. Set **Repeat until** to the end of the term.
9. Review the target, dates, and required signers.
10. Create the schedule.

![Recurring Task Settings](/images/recurring_tasks.png)

| Setting | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| **Start date** | Usually the academic term start or the first reporting period |
| **Frequency** | Matches the program's actual sign-off cadence |
| **Due date** | Gives every required signer enough time |
| **Repeat until** | Prevents assignments from continuing after the term |
| **Target** | Correct cohort or selected individuals |
| **Signers** | Correct student, site supervisor, faculty, and custom roles |

## A schedule is a generator

A recurring schedule is not itself a student assignment. It creates a new assignment on each scheduled date.

Before the first run:

- Look under the cohort's **Assignments → Schedules** segment
- Do not expect a future assignment to appear in the normal assignment list yet
- Confirm the next run date and end date

After the first run:

- Confirm that the emitted assignment reached the correct students
- Open one item and verify its due date and signer sequence
- Check the submissions view to see who is currently responsible

## Stopping or changing a schedule

You can stop a recurring schedule from the cohort's **Schedules** segment. Stopping it prevents future assignments from being generated; assignments already created remain available.

If the cadence or date range is wrong, stop the schedule and create the corrected one. Review already-emitted assignments separately so students do not receive duplicates.

## Term rollover

Do not assume an hour-log schedule copied into a new cohort. When creating the next term:

1. Create the cohort using **Copy from existing** or a template cohort.
2. Verify the copied one-time assignments and shifted due dates.
3. Open **Schedules** in the new cohort.
4. Recreate the weekly hour-log schedule with the new term's start and end dates.
5. Confirm the first generated assignment after the schedule runs.

See [Next Semester Setup](/fieldx/step-5-next-semester-setup) for the complete rollover checklist.
