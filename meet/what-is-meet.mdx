---
title: "What is Meet?"
description: "Meet is FieldX's built-in tool for scheduling, recording, and reviewing HIPAA-compliant supervision sessions."
---

Meet lets you schedule and record supervision sessions right inside FieldX. It works a lot like a video conferencing tool you may already know, but it's built for clinical training: every session is HIPAA-compliant, and recordings stay stored on your FieldX account.

## Where to find it

From FieldX, click the **Meet** button to open the Supervision Room. This is your hub for scheduling, recording, and reviewing meetings.

<Note>
  Meet turns on per account. If you don't see a Meet button, check with your Program Admin.
</Note>

## The three tabs

The Supervision Room is organized into three tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Meetings you've scheduled, or meetings someone else has invited you to |
| **Past sessions** | Recordings of meetings you've finished, plus any you've uploaded yourself |
| **Shared with you** | Sessions other people on the platform have shared with you |
