---
title: "What is Meet?"
description: "Meet is FieldX's built-in tool for scheduling, recording, and reviewing supervision sessions, built with HIPAA and FERPA safeguards."
---

Meet lets you schedule and record supervision sessions right inside FieldX. It works a lot like a video conferencing tool you may already know, but it's built for clinical training. Sessions can involve real clients, so Meet asks for consent before anything is transcribed, limits who can watch a recording, and keeps a record of every view. See [Privacy & security](/meet/privacy-and-security) for the details.

## Where to find it

From FieldX, click the **Meet** button to open the Supervision Room. This is your hub for scheduling, recording, and reviewing meetings.

## The tabs

The Supervision Room is organized into tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Meetings you've scheduled, or meetings someone else has invited you to |
| **Past sessions** | Recordings of meetings you've finished, plus any you've uploaded yourself |
| **Shared with you** | Sessions other people on the platform have shared with you |
| **Program Oversight** | Program admins only. [Manage your students' recordings](/meet/program-oversight) |
