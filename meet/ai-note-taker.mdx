---
title: "The AI Note Taker"
description: "Meet includes an optional, HIPAA-compliant AI note taker that transcribes and summarizes your session."
---

Before you join a meeting, you can turn on the AI note taker. It works similarly to other AI note-taking tools you may have used, but it's HIPAA-compliant and built for supervision sessions.

## What it does

While the meeting is happening, the note taker listens in and builds a transcript of what's said. Once the meeting ends, it generates a summary of the session.

<Note>
  It can take a few minutes for the transcript and summary to finish generating after a meeting ends. If they're not showing up yet on the [Past Sessions](/meet/past-sessions) page, check back shortly.
</Note>

## Giving consent

Before the note taker can record, you'll be asked to give consent. Confirming consent is also what drops you into the meeting itself.

<Tip>
  Since these are supervision recordings, it's good practice to let everyone joining know ahead of time that the session will be recorded.
</Tip>

## What's next

Once you're in the meeting, see [Participants & Invites](/meet/participants-and-invites) to bring other people in, or [Past Sessions](/meet/past-sessions) to see what happens once you end the call.
