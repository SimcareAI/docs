---
title: "Hiding Identifying Details"
description: "Replace names, dates, places, and contact details in a transcript so reviewers and AI don't see them."
---

A session can have its transcript **redacted**. Names, dates, places, contact details, and other identifiers are replaced with placeholders for everyone reviewing it, AI feedback included. The person who recorded the session, the owner, can still see the originals.

<Warning>
  Redaction is not full de-identification. Automatic detection can miss nicknames, unusual names, and mumbled speech. The audio and video still contain everything that was said, so names are still audible.
</Warning>

## Turn it on

| Where | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Program default | Program admin | **Program Management**, then **Student Visibility**, or **Meet**, then **Settings** |
| One session | The host | Turn on **Hide identifying details** on the pre-join screen, or on the upload form |

New sessions start from the program default. Scheduling a meeting for someone else uses the host's program default.

## What reviewers see

- Identifiers become consistent placeholders, such as **[NAME_1]**. The same name always gets the same placeholder.
- Speakers appear as **Counselor** or **Speaker 2**, not real names.
- Medical terms such as medications, doses, and conditions stay visible.
- Lines that were never scanned show **[Not yet redacted]** and their text stays hidden.

This applies to graders, peers, admins, and faculty, and to AI feedback.

## What the owner can do

- Turn on **Show identifying details** to see the original text. Each time you do, it's recorded in the audit log.
- Hide a phrase the detector missed, everywhere it appears.
- Restore something that was hidden by mistake.

If you re-transcribe the recording, the lines are replaced and your corrections are lost.

## Things to know

- Only the owner can rename speakers on a redacted session.
- If you turn redaction on for a session that already has a live transcript, its lines show **[Not yet redacted]** until you re-transcribe from the recording.
- Redaction doesn't cover text people type, such as comments.
