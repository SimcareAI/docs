---

Once you're in a meeting, you'll see everyone currently on the call.

## Inviting others

There are a couple of ways to bring someone into a meeting:

- **Share the link or room code.** Send either one directly to the person you want to join.
- **Send an email invite from inside Meet.** Add their email, and they'll get an invite in their inbox with a link to join.

## If you scheduled ahead of time

When you [schedule a meeting](/meet/scheduling-and-starting-a-meeting), you can add participants as part of setting it up. If you created the meeting, you can also add more people or edit the participant list later.

<Tip>
  If you and someone else are together in person, you don't need two separate devices. You can share one camera to record both of you in the same session.
</Tip>
