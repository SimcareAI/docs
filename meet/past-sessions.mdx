When you end a meeting, it moves into the **Past sessions** tab of the Supervision Room.

## The past sessions list

Each session shows:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The name of the session — you can rename it |
| **Meeting Code** | The room code for that session |
| **Participants** | Who was on the call |
| **Shares** | How many times the recording has been shared |
| **Date & Time** | When the session happened |

## Viewing a session

Click **View Details** on any session to open it. From there, you can:

- Rename the session
- View the transcript and AI summary once the [note taker](/meet/ai-note-taker) finishes processing
- Generate AI feedback on the session, similar to the feedback SimCare generates for Simulation practice sessions

<Note>
  Notes take a little time to generate after a meeting ends. If a session's transcript or summary isn't showing up yet, check back in a few minutes.
</Note>
