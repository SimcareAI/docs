When you end a meeting, it moves into the **Past sessions** tab of the Supervision Room.

## The past sessions list

Use the search box to find a session by title, room code, or owner. Switch between card and table views using the controls beside the sort menu. Click the title to rename a session you own.

## Opening a session

Click the **⋮** menu on a session and choose **Details**. From there, you can:

- Watch the recording
- Rename the session
- Read the live transcript and AI notes, if the [note taker](/meet/ai-note-taker) was on (owner only)
- Share the session, add documents, and leave comments
- Select **Transcript** to read the transcript and add [time-stamped comments](/meet/reviewing-a-session)

<Note>
  Transcripts and notes take a little time to generate after a meeting ends. If one isn't showing up yet, check back in a few minutes.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Generate Feedback** is temporarily unavailable while we improve it. The **Transcript** panel and comments work as usual.
</Note>
