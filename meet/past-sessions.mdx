When you end a meeting, it moves into the **Past sessions** tab of the Supervision Room.

## The past sessions list

Each session shows its title, meeting code, participants, date and time, and how many times it has been shared. Click the title to rename a session you own.

## Opening a session

Click the **⋮** menu on a session and choose **Details**. From there, you can:

- Watch the recording
- Rename the session
- Read the live transcript and AI notes, if the [note taker](/meet/ai-note-taker) was on (owner only)
- Share the session, add documents, and leave comments
- Open **Review session** to read the transcript and add [time-stamped comments](/meet/reviewing-a-session)

<Note>
  Transcripts and notes take a little time to generate after a meeting ends. If one isn't showing up yet, check back in a few minutes.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Generate Feedback** is temporarily unavailable while we improve it. The transcript and comments in **Review session** work as usual.
</Note>

## What's next

See [Reviewing a session](/meet/reviewing-a-session) for the transcript and comments, or [Sharing, Documents & Comments](/meet/sharing-documents-and-comments) to send a recording to someone else.
