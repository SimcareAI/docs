---
title: "Scheduling & Starting a Meeting"
description: "Set up a Meet session ahead of time, start one right now, or schedule one for someone else."
---

There are two ways to set up a Meet session, depending on whether you want to plan ahead or get started right away. Program admins can also schedule a session for someone else.

## Schedule a meeting for later

Click **Schedule Meeting** at the top of the Supervision Room. Choose a title, date and time, and a length of 30 to 120 minutes. Add up to 20 people by email. Each invitee gets an email with a calendar invite. Once it's created, the meeting shows up under the **Scheduled** tab.

<Note>
  Only the person who created a scheduled meeting can edit its details or add more people to it afterward.
</Note>

### The lobby

If someone arrives before the host, they wait in a lobby. When the host opens the room, everyone waiting is let in automatically.

## Start a meeting right now

If you don't need to schedule anything, click **New Meeting** to start a session immediately.

You'll be asked for a few basic settings first, like your name for the session. From there, you can turn on the [AI note taker](/meet/ai-note-taker) and [hide identifying details](/meet/privacy-and-redaction) before you join.

## Schedule a meeting for someone else

Program admins can schedule a session for a member of their program:

1. Click **Schedule Meeting**.
2. Pick the person who will host from the **Host** field.
3. Finish the details and schedule it.

The host owns and runs the session. You're invited too, and you keep access to its recordings. Everyone is told this before they agree to be recorded: the consent text names you and says you'll also have access to the recording. The host confirms this once before they can enter the room.

<Note>
  The **Host** field only appears if you're a program admin or a FieldX coordinator.
</Note>

## What's next

Once your meeting is set up, see [The AI Note Taker](/meet/ai-note-taker) to learn what it captures, or [Participants & Invites](/meet/participants-and-invites) to bring other people into the session.
