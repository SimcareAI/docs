---
title: "Sharing, Documents & Comments"
description: "Share a recording with someone else on the platform, add supporting documents, and leave comments."
---

From a session's detail page, you can share it, add documents to it, and leave comments directly on the recording.

## Sharing a session

Only the person who owns a session can share it. Enter the email of anyone else on the platform to share it with them. They'll get a notification, and the session will show up under their **Shared with you** tab.

To stop sharing, remove the person from the session's **Shared with** list. If a session is [redacted](/meet/privacy-and-redaction), the people you share it with see the redacted transcript only.

## Adding documents

You can upload documents onto a session page — for example, reading material that goes along with a recording. This is useful for anything supplemental you want attached to that specific session.

## Leaving comments

You can leave comments on a session from its detail page. For comments pinned to a moment in the video, use [Review session](/meet/reviewing-a-session).

<Warning>
  Don't type client names or other identifying details into comments. Redaction doesn't cover text that people type.
</Warning>
