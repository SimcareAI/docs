title: "Program Courses"
description: "Program-admin view of every course in your program, grouped by instructor, with read-only access to classes you don’t own."
---

**Program Courses** lists every class in the programs you administer, not just the ones you teach. Instructors are grouped as owners so you can see who is running what.

Open it from **Courses** in the top navigation, then click **Program Courses** (`/classes?tab=program-courses`).

<Note>
  This tab only appears if you have program-admin or teacher-admin access in an LMS program. Regular instructors will not see it. Deep links to `?tab=program-courses` fall back to **My Courses** without that role.
</Note>

This tab is oversight of **live classes**. To build reusable course *templates* (modules, cases, quizzes) that aren’t tied to one instructor’s roster, use [Curriculum builder](/simulations/curriculum-builder).

## Browsing

Courses are grouped by owner. Your own courses appear first, labeled **(you)**. Other groups show the instructor’s name and email.

Each card works like [My Courses](/simulations/my-courses): name, enrollment, and (when you can manage that course) edit and delete.

Use **Search by course or owner...** to filter by course name, description, or instructor. If your program has a very large catalog, SimCare may show only the most recent courses until you refine the search.

If you belong to more than one program, a program name chip appears on cards so you can tell them apart.

## Opening a course you don’t own

Click a card to open that class. If you are **not** the owner, the class opens in a **read-only** oversight view: you can see roster, assignments, and progress, but you cannot invite students, change roles, or edit assignments.

If you **are** the owner, you get the same full access as from **My Courses**.

**Back** returns you to **Program Courses**, not **My Courses**.

## Managing a course from this tab

On courses you’re allowed to manage, the card menu still supports **Edit class** (rename / description) and delete. Duplicate and Canvas export are on **My Courses**, for classes in your own list.

<Tip>
  To give an instructor a copy of a course they can teach, have them duplicate from **My Courses** (if they own it) or clone a template from the [Course catalog](/simulations/course-catalog).
</Tip>
