**My Courses** is your personal assignments dashboard. It shows every class you're enrolled in, every assignment that's been given to you, and your progress across all of them.

Access it by clicking **Courses** in the top navigation. This opens **My Learning Dashboard**.

<Warning>
  **Practicing here vs. doing an assignment:** Talking to a client through **Simulations** or **Presenting Issues** is unlimited sandbox practice, it is never graded and never visible to your instructor, even if it's the exact same client as one of your assignments. Only sessions started from an assignment inside **My Courses** (via **Start Assignment**, below) count toward your coursework.
</Warning>

## Layout

My Courses has three sections:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | Use **Upcoming** to see upcoming assignments and **Grades** to view grades. |
| **My Courses** | Classes your instructor has enrolled you in, with a progress bar and next due assignment. |
| **Personal Learning** | Self-paced courses you enrolled in yourself from the [Course Catalog](/simulations/course-catalog). |

## Assignment status badges

Each assignment card shows a status badge:

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not Started** | You haven't opened the assignment yet |
| **In Progress** | You started a session but haven't submitted |
| **Submitted** | Your session is complete and awaiting review |
| **Graded** | Your instructor has reviewed and saved a grade |
| **Locked** | Complete and submit the prerequisite assignment shown in the row to unlock this assignment |
| **Not Submitted** | No submission has been recorded for this assignment |
| **Past Due** | The due date passed without a submission |

Check your grades any time under the **Grades** tab in Activity.

## Starting an assignment

1. Find the assignment in **Activity** or inside a class in **My Courses**
2. Click **Start Assignment** for the case assignment.
3. Review **Patient info**, **Assignment Details**, and the **Rubric** if one is configured, then click **Continue to Case Overview**.
4. Review **Simulation Overview** and click **Start Conversation**. Choose an avatar mode if prompted, then complete **Case Setup**. If you enabled Text Input Mode, use **Proceed with Text Input** instead of microphone setup.

<Note>
  If your instructor set an **attempt limit**, the number of remaining attempts is shown on the assignment card. Once you've used all attempts, the Start button is disabled.
</Note>

## Resuming a session

If you close the browser mid-session, your progress is saved. Return to the assignment and click **Continue** — the session picks up where you left off.

## Viewing feedback

Once feedback is released:

1. Open the assignment in My Courses
2. Click **View Feedback**
3. Your feedback report opens with the sections enabled for that assignment. Labels can include **Chat with Feedback**, **Annotated Transcript**, **Evaluation Checklist**, and **Advanced Skills**; not every assignment includes every report type.

<Note>
  If you see "Your instructor will release feedback" instead of the feedback report, your instructor has chosen to review before releasing. You'll get a notification when it's available.
</Note>

## Assignment types

Besides simulation cases, your instructor may assign:

| Type | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Case** | Complete a simulation session with an AI client |
| **Quiz** | Answer multiple choice, true/false, or written questions |
| **Document** | Read a PDF/URL or upload a required file |
| **Form** | Complete a multi-step form (evaluations, reflections) |

## Common issues

**I can't find an assignment my instructor mentioned.**<br />Check all three sections (Activity, My Courses, Personal Learning). If it's a class-based assignment, click into the class card under My Courses. If you still can't find it, ask your instructor to confirm you're enrolled in the class.

**The Start button is greyed out.**<br />If the assignment is **Locked**, check the row for the prerequisite assignment you must submit first. You may also have used all your allowed attempts, or the assignment's due date has passed. Check the assignment card for details or ask your instructor.

**My submission shows "In Progress" but I finished the session.**<br />The session may still be processing. Wait a few minutes and refresh the page. If it stays stuck after 10 minutes, contact [hello@simcare.ai](mailto:hello@simcare.ai).
