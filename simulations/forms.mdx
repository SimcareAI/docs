The **Forms** tab on **Courses** is a faculty list of every form you can use in simulation classes: forms you created, plus forms shared with your programs.

Open it from **Courses** in the top navigation, then click **Forms** (`/classes?tab=forms`).

This is the simulation-class forms list. Field placement evaluations and site paperwork live in FieldX — see [Building forms](/fieldx/building-forms) if that’s what you need.

## What’s on the list

The heading explains the scope: **Forms you own and forms shared with your programs.**

Each row shows:

- Form title and description
- How many **assignments** and **submissions** use it
- A chip: **Owned** (you created it) or **Shared** (your program shared it with you)

Click a row to open that form at `/forms-and-reports/forms/...`.

Use **Search forms…** to filter by title or description.

## Create a form

Click **Create New Form** (top right). That opens the form builder at `/forms-and-reports/forms/new`.

If you have no forms yet, the empty state offers **Create your first form**.

<Tip>
  After a form exists, you attach it to a class as a form assignment from inside that class — not from this tab. Open the class from [My Courses](/simulations/my-courses), then create an assignment.
</Tip>
