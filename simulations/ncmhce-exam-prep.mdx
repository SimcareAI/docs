NCMHCE Exam Prep is a dedicated dashboard for working through clinical case studies in the NCMHCE format. Each case pairs a written clinical scenario with multiple-choice questions, and most cases also offer a paired AI simulation so you can talk with the client before answering.

Open it from the **NCMHCE Exam Prep** tab in your SimCare dashboard.

## What's on the dashboard

The Exam Prep home page has three main areas:

- **Simulations with NCMHCE questions** — a featured carousel of cases you can launch as a live simulation. Each card shows the patient image, case name, and question count.
- **Case study library** — every available case, numbered (**Case Study 1**, **Case Study 2**, …) and tagged with its question count. Cases beyond the free preview are marked **Premium**.
- **View all Study Packages** and **Upgrade to Full Access** — open the study-package picker or upgrade your plan from the top bar.

## Two ways to take a case

Click any case to open the **Case Overview** modal. It shows the patient name, age, and clinical background, plus one or two ways to start:

### Start case study (direct)

Jumps straight to the written case and the NCMHCE-style questions. Use this when you want to focus on assessment, diagnosis, and treatment-planning questions without a live conversation first.

### Try simulated case study instead

Available on any case with a paired AI client. You'll first run a full SimCare simulation with the client, then the NCMHCE questions for that case open automatically after the session ends. Use this when you want realistic intake practice before testing your clinical reasoning.

<Note>The simulated path uses the same scoring and review screens as the direct path — the only difference is the conversation that comes before it.</Note>

## Answering questions

Inside a case:

- A **stepper** at the top lets you jump between questions.
- Your answers **auto-save** as you go, so you can leave and come back from the dashboard without losing progress.
- Click **Submit** on the last question to lock in your attempt and generate a score.

After you submit, the case switches to **review mode**: you see your final score (e.g., `Final Score: 7 / 10`), the correct answer for each question, and any rationale included with the case.

## Resuming or retaking a case

- Returning to an in-progress case picks up where you left off.
- Returning to a completed case opens it in review mode so you can re-read explanations.
- To start a fresh attempt, launch the case again from the dashboard — a new attempt is created.

## Study packages

Click **View all Study Packages** to browse curated bundles of cases (for example, full-length practice exams or topic-focused sets). Selecting a package filters the library to the cases included in that bundle.
