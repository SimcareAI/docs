Once you've logged in, it's time to start your first conversation with an AI client.

<Warning>
  **Practicing here vs. doing an assignment:** Starting a session from Discover, Presenting Issues, or any other browse page (as described below) is unlimited sandbox practice. It is **not graded and not visible to your instructor**, even if you pick the exact same client as one of your assignments. If you want a session to count toward your coursework, start it from the assignment itself inside **My Courses** instead. See [My Learning](/simulations/my-learning) for how to find and start your assigned work.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the clients">
    When you log in, you'll land on the **Discover page**. Here, you'll see:

    - A range of **AI client cards**, each with a different background and presenting issue (like anxiety or family stress)
    - Clients grouped by category to help you choose

    Take your time, read the short descriptions, and pick a client that interests you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the clients">
    When you log in, you'll land on the **Discover page**. Here you'll see:

    - A **feature banner** ("Build your own clients, instantly ✨") if you have access to the [Client Builder](/simulations/client-builder)
    - **Trending Simulations** — popular cases right now
    - **Collections for Your Course** — curated bundles like Multicultural Counseling, Counseling Theories & Techniques, Career Counseling, Group Counseling, Crisis & Trauma, Child & Adolescent, Addiction, and Family Systems
    - **Browse by Presenting Issue** — filter cards (e.g., anxiety, family stress, history-taking)
    - **Faculty Created Clients** and **Moving Avatars** rows
    - **Browse by Population** — age-based filtering (Children & Adolescents, Young Adult, Adult, Senior)
    - **More Than One Voice in the Room** — group cases with multiple avatars

    Take your time, read the short descriptions, and pick a client that interests you. If you're not sure where to start, click **Match me with a client** at the bottom of the page — the AI will suggest a client based on what you want to practice.

    <Tip>
      **Hover to preview:** Cards for cases with a moving avatar play a short animated preview when you hover over them, so you can get a feel for the client before you commit.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Start Case"'>
    When you're ready, click the **"Start Case"** button on any client card.

    <Tip>
      **Recommendation:** A pop-up will suggest finding a **quiet space** where you can speak freely. This helps make the conversation feel more natural and ensures the AI hears you clearly.
    </Tip>

    Click **Continue** once you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a case and customize">
    When you're ready, click any client card. A **Case customization** popup opens with two options:

    - **Quick Start** — use the default prompt and begin immediately
    - **Generate & Start** — write a short prompt to nudge the case toward a specific learning goal

    <Tip>
      A pop-up will suggest finding a **quiet space** where you can speak freely. This helps make the conversation feel more natural and ensures the AI hears you clearly.
    </Tip>

    Click **Continue** once you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the orientation card">
    Before the session begins, you'll see an **orientation card** with a short blurb about the client. This gives you key background information to help you prepare.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an avatar mode (some cases)">
    If the case supports a moving avatar, you'll see a **Select Mode** screen with two side-by-side previews:

    - **Static Avatar** — a still image of the client throughout the session
    - **Moving Avatar** — an AI-animated version of the client that lip-syncs and reacts as they speak

    Cases that support this show a **Moving Avatar** chip on the case card in Discover, so you know before you click in.

    Select the mode you want to use for this session. Your choice only applies to this run — you can pick again next time you start the case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the orientation card">
    Before the session begins, you'll see a **Case Overview** with a short blurb about the client, their background, and any supporting documents you can preview or download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on video (optional)">
    You have full control over your camera settings:

    - **By default, your camera is off.**
    - If you want the client to see your face, you can **turn your camera on**.
    - This is optional — totally up to you.

    When you are ready, click **"Start Conversation"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the intro (some cases)">
    A small number of cases include a short orientation video. If yours does, it plays at the **Video Audio Check** step before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audio & video check">
    You'll see a video and audio setup page where you can:

    - Test your microphone
    - Preview your camera (if it's on)
    - Check your speaker output

    <Warning>
      **Browser permissions:** If it's your first session, your browser (Chrome or Edge) will ask for permission to use your mic and camera. Be sure to click **Allow** when prompted — otherwise, the simulation won't work.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your mic, camera, and speakers">
    On the **Case Setup** screen you can:

    - Select your **microphone** from the dropdown and watch the live audio level bar move as you speak
    - Select your **camera** (camera is off by default — turning it on lets the client "see" you)
    - Select your **speaker** and click the round purple button to play a sample sound

    <Warning>
      **Browser permissions:** If it's your first session, your browser (Chrome or Edge) will ask for permission to use your mic and camera. Click **Allow** when prompted — otherwise the simulation won't work. If you accidentally blocked it, the page shows a permission error with a **Try Again** button.
    </Warning>

    ![Browser permission prompt for microphone and camera](/images/first_session_6.webp)

    When everything looks right, click **Proceed**. (If your mic isn't working the button reads **Fix Microphone to Continue** and is disabled; if your camera isn't working it reads **Proceed Without Camera**.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unmute to begin">
    <Warning>
      **Important:** The client can't hear you until you **unmute yourself**.
    </Warning>

    Unlike other tools, **there is no "Submit" button**.

    - SimCare automatically listens while you speak.
    - The client responds naturally when you are done speaking.
    - You can also **interrupt the client** naturally, just like in a real session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unmute to begin">
    <Warning>
      **Important:** The client can't hear you until you **unmute yourself**. Your mic is also briefly locked while the avatar gives its opening line — once they finish, your mic unlocks automatically.
    </Warning>

    Unlike other tools, **there is no "Submit" button**.

    - SimCare automatically listens while you speak
    - The client responds naturally when you're done speaking
    - You can also **interrupt the client** naturally, just like in a real session
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you've unmuted, your session has officially started. Start talking — the client is ready to listen.

## Resuming a session

If you close the tab or refresh by accident, your session is preserved. Return to the same case from your dashboard and SimCare will pick up where you left off — no progress lost.
