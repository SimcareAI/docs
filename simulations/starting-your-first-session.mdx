Once you've logged in, it's time to start your first conversation with an AI client.

<Warning>
  **Practicing here vs. doing an assignment:** Starting a session from Simulations, Presenting Issues, or any other browse page (as described below) is unlimited sandbox practice. It is **not graded and not visible to your instructor**, even if you pick the exact same client as one of your assignments. If you want a session to count toward your coursework, start it from the assignment itself inside **Courses** instead. See [My Learning](/simulations/my-learning) for how to find and start your assigned work.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the clients">
    Open **Simulations** to browse clients. Here you'll see:

    - **Full Session** and **Focused Scenarios** tabs
    - Search by client name and **Filters**
    - **Trending clients**, **Browse by presenting issue**, and **Weekly spotlights**
    - **Browse by Population**, **Course Catalog**, and **Group simulations**
    - **Create Custom Client** if your account has builder access

    Read the client descriptions and choose a case. To get a recommendation, click **Match Me to a Client** beside the search and filter controls, describe what you want to practice, and submit.

    <Tip>
      **Hover to preview:** Cards for cases with a moving avatar play a short animated preview when you hover over them, so you can get a feel for the client before you commit.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a case and customize">
    Click a client card to open **Case customization**. For a full practice session, stay on **Full Session**, choose the **Session Type** and **Client Stance**, then click **Start Session**. The popup also includes **Focused Scenarios** and **History** tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the orientation card">
    Before the session begins, you'll see a **Simulation Overview** with a short blurb about the client, their background, and any supporting documents you can preview or download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on video (optional)">
    You have full control over your camera settings:

    - **By default, your camera is off.**
    - If you want the client to see your face, you can **turn your camera on**.
    - This is optional — totally up to you.

    When you are ready, click **"Start Conversation"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an avatar mode (some cases)">
    If the case supports a moving avatar, you'll see a **Select Mode** screen with two side-by-side previews:

    - **Static Avatar** — a still image of the client throughout the session
    - **Moving Avatar** — an AI-animated version of the client that lip-syncs and reacts as they speak

    Cases that support this show a **Moving Avatar** chip on the case card in Simulations, so you know before you click in.

    Select the mode you want to use for this session. Your choice only applies to this run — you can pick again next time you start the case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the intro (some cases)">
    A small number of cases include a short orientation video. If yours does, it plays at the **Video Audio Check** step before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your mic, camera, and speakers">
    On the **Case Setup** screen you can:

    - Select your **microphone** from the dropdown and watch the live audio level bar move as you speak
    - Select your **camera** (camera is off by default — turning it on lets the client "see" you)
    - Select your **speaker** and click the round purple button to play a sample sound

    Find a quiet space where you can speak freely, as recommended on this screen.

    <Warning>
      **Browser permissions:** Voice input requires microphone access. Allow microphone access when prompted to use voice input; camera access is needed if you enable video. If you accidentally blocked it, the page shows a permission error with a **Try Again** button.
    </Warning>

    ![Browser permission prompt for microphone and camera](/images/first_session_6.webp)

    When everything looks right, click **Proceed**. (If your mic isn't working the button reads **Fix Microphone to Continue** and is disabled; if your camera isn't working it reads **Proceed Without Camera**.)

    To type instead of speaking, enable **Text Input Mode** in **Account Settings → Accessibility** before starting. On **Text input setup**, click **Proceed with Text Input**. Microphone access is not required in this mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unmute to begin (voice input)">
    <Warning>
      **Important:** The client can't hear you until you **unmute yourself**. Your mic is also briefly locked while the avatar gives its opening line — once they finish, your mic unlocks automatically.
    </Warning>

    Unlike other tools, **there is no "Submit" button**.

    - SimCare automatically listens while you speak
    - The client responds naturally when you're done speaking
    - You can also **interrupt the client** naturally, just like in a real session
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you've unmuted, your session has officially started. Start talking — the client is ready to listen.

## Resuming a session

If you close the tab or refresh by accident, your session is preserved. Return to the same case from your dashboard and SimCare will pick up where you left off — no progress lost.
