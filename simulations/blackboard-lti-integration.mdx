The SimCare LTI integration for Blackboard lets instructors add a SimCare launch link and SimCare assignments to a Blackboard course, launch cases in test mode, and receive student feedback for review inside SimCare.

<Note>
  This same workflow applies to **FieldX**. The steps below use SimCare as the example, but Blackboard integration works identically for FieldX assignments and launches.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Prefer to watch? See the full walkthrough on [Loom](https://loom.com/share/70b8e87e588b40859b34bc9aaaae0bed).
</Tip>

## Add the Launch SimCare link

Give instructors and students a one-click way to open SimCare from inside the Blackboard course.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Content Market">
    In your Blackboard course, click **Add Content** and open the **Content Market**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Launch SimCare">
    Choose the SimCare option labeled **Launch SimCare**. This creates a simple link that lets instructors or students open SimCare in a new tab and be automatically logged in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the course">
    Refresh the Blackboard course page to confirm the new launch link appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a SimCare assignment

Pull an existing SimCare assignment into the Blackboard course through the Content Market.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Content Market">
    Go back to **Add Content** and open the **Content Market** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose SimCare Assignment Picker">
    Select **SimCare Assignment Picker**. The picker shows every assignment tied to your program, including ones created by instructors, course builders, subject matter experts, or others in the program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an assignment">
    Pick the assignment you want to use (for example, **Assignment 3**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the course">
    Refresh the Blackboard page to confirm the assignment link has been added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Launch as an instructor to test

Verify the assignment flow before students start.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the assignment link">
    Open the assignment link you just added in Blackboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Runs in test mode">
    The assignment opens in a new tab and launches in **test mode** for the instructor. For a case study, you'll be asked to choose a client and can step through the assignment exactly as a student would.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Student workflow

From the student side of the same Blackboard course, students see both the launch link and the assignment link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch SimCare">
    Clicking **Launch SimCare** logs the student into SimCare in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the assignment">
    Clicking the assignment link opens the actual assignment so the student can begin work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the case conversation">
    The student must complete at least **four back-and-forth exchanges** with the client for the case to count. Once the conversation is sufficiently complete, the student can end the case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the call">
    Ending the call marks the assignment as completed and starts feedback generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Feedback and instructor review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Feedback is generated">
    After the student ends the conversation, SimCare generates feedback and sends it back to the instructor side for review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the class in SimCare">
    On the SimCare instructor side, a new class appears with the same name as the Blackboard course. The assignment copied from the master course is linked to the Blackboard course launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the submission">
    Open the student's submission (for example, **John Doe**) and click **View Submissions**, then **Grade a Case** to review the work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Grading details are handled in SimCare, not directly in Blackboard.
</Note>

## Grade passback

Grade passback is not yet fully covered by this workflow. The intended flow is:

1. Instructor enters a grade in SimCare.
2. Instructor clicks **Publish Grade**.
3. Grade passback sends the score back to Blackboard.

<Warning>
  The team is still investigating a zeroing issue and the full Blackboard Ultra grade passback workflow. Contact support if you hit issues with grades not appearing in Blackboard.
</Warning>

## Same flow for FieldX

Everything above works the same way for **FieldX**. Use the **Launch FieldX** link and the **FieldX Assignment Picker** in the Blackboard Content Market to add launches and assignments, and students and instructors follow the identical launch, completion, and review flow.
